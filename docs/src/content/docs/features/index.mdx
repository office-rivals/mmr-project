These pages describe the beta frontend in [`frontend-beta/`](https://github.com/office-rivals/mmr-project/tree/main/frontend-beta). The SvelteKit app in `frontend/` still exists alongside it, and both talk to the same V3 API.

## Feature areas

- **Leaderboards and seasons.** Each league has a leaderboard ranked by MMR, a feed of recent matches with each player's rating change, and a season picker for past seasons. You need 10 matches in a season to be ranked. Until then you're listed under "Unranked", with 🐣 in place of your MMR. A streak of three or more wins shows 🔥, and three or more losses show 🌧️ (⛈️ from seven).
- **Matchmaking queue.** Queue up in a league. Once enough players are waiting to fill both teams, the app proposes balanced teams and everyone has about 30 seconds to accept. See [Matchmaking](/features/matchmaking/).
- **Match submission.** Record a 1v1 or 2v2 result with existing league players, organization members who haven't joined the league yet, or new players you create on the spot. A league uses either fixed-target scoring (the winner reaches an exact score, 10 by default) or free-form scoring (you enter both scores and the higher one wins). See [Match submission](/features/match-submission/).
- **Random teams.** `/random` splits four names into two teams of two. On a touch screen it can also pick teams from fingers placed on the screen.
- **Player profiles and statistics.** A player page shows rank, MMR, match count, wins, losses, win rate and current streak, plus a rating-over-time chart, most common opponents and teammates, and match history. The league statistics page at `/<org>/<league>/statistics` charts every player's rating over time and shows when matches are played.
- **Organizations, leagues and invites.** Members join an organization through an invite link or an email invite, then join whichever leagues they like. See [Organizations and leagues](/features/organizations-and-leagues/).
- **Roles.** Every organization member is an Owner, a Moderator or a Member. Owners and moderators get the admin area at `/admin`. See [Admin operations](/features/admin-operations/).
- **Match flags and moderation.** League players report a match they think is wrong. Moderators and owners correct the match, recalculate ratings, and resolve or dismiss the flag.
- **Personal access tokens.** Create and revoke tokens for scripted API access from your settings. See [Access control and tokens](/features/access-control-and-tokens/).

## Tenant model

Access is checked at two levels:

- organization membership controls access to the tenant
- league membership controls a regular member's access to data inside a league

Roles belong to the organization, not to a league. Moderators and owners can manage every league in their organization without joining it. Regular members must join a league before they can read or write its data.

## Security boundaries

- Non-admins can't read or write league data for leagues they haven't joined.
- Nobody can read or write data in an organization they aren't an active member of.
- Moderators and owners have organization-wide access by design, but no access to other organizations.
- Personal access tokens act as the user who created them and can be restricted further to one organization or league.

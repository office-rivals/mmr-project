# Matchmaking

Players arrange league games through matchmaking or game invites.

## Language

**Game invite**:
A private, league-specific invitation that gathers players for a game through a shared link. Its creator is also a participant.
_Avoid_: Organization invite, pending match

**Invite participant**:
A league player occupying a spot in a game invite, whether they joined themselves or were added by another participant.

**Invite creator**:
The participant who started a game invite and can cancel it or remove participants while the group is waiting.

**Player representative**:
An invite participant responsible for managing another player's spot and responding to match acceptance on their behalf. This is initially the participant who added the player, and may transfer to the invite creator.

**Organization invite**:
An invitation to become a member of an organization.
_Avoid_: Game invite

**Pending match**:
A proposed match with assigned teams, awaiting the players' acceptance before play begins.
_Avoid_: Game invite

**Active match**:
A match accepted by all participants and awaiting a result or cancellation.

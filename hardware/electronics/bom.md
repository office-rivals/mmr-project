### Suppliers: record the exact variant at checkout

The clone parts have no stable part number. When you order, save the listing URL, the variant name and a
photo of **both sides** of the board in the order notes, and check them on arrival:

| Part | Must have | Reject if |
|---|---|---|
| LIS3DH clone | chip marked **C3H**; INT1, SDA, SCL, CS, SDO pins; a 3.3V pin or no regulator; I²C pull-ups to that 3.3 V | a LIS3DSH, VCC only through a regulator, no INT1 pin |
| PAM8302A clone | SD pin; input caps on A+/A−; ceramic VIN bypass; printed pin order | no SD pin |
| USB-C panel extension | 90° in-plane male, 17 mm ear pitch, CC passed: check a C-to-C laptop cable powers the XIAO and the port enumerates **in both plug orientations** | a charge-only or "USB 2.0 power" cable that fails either orientation |

**Cutting the window and the diffuser at home** (keep the protective film on until it's fitted; mark on the film):
- **Acrylic (PMMA), 2 mm:** score + snap. Clamp a steel ruler on the line and draw an acrylic scoring
  knife (or the back of a utility-knife blade) along it 6–8 times, about a third of the way through.
  Then lay the score over a table edge and snap it down in one firm move. Sand the edge with 240 grit.
- **Polycarbonate (the 3 mm window, the 2 mm opal bar):** it bends instead of snapping, so **saw it**, or ask the shop to cut to size. Use a fine-tooth hacksaw
  (32 TPI), or a jigsaw with a fine metal blade at low speed. Clamp it between two boards with masking

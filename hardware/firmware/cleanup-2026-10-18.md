## Cut

1. **The scoreboard:** `src/scoreboard/` (render, panel, speaker, io, fonts.h, samples.h), `include/scoreboard/`,
   `lib/core/scoreboard*`, `ui_text.h`, `test/test_scoreboard/` and the 11 scoreboard app tests, `tools/make_fonts.py`,
   `make_sounds.py`, `tools/preview/`, the 4 scoreboard envs, and the GxEPD2 / QRCode / MFRC522 deps.
2. **Scoreboard-only code in shared files:**
   - Hal: `show`, `sample`, `leds`, `readCard`, `readerOff`, `panelBusy`, `wifiFastRx`.
   - The long-poll lane (`Lane::Poll`), Purpose Action/Poll, the speaker path, RtcState's screen cache.
   - `Config.end`, `Resource.role/end`, the `role::Role` enum, the Won/Joined/Saved sounds and the Swell shape.
3. **Merged layers:**
   - `AppBase` had one subclass left, so it is folded into `GoalApp` (`app_base.*` deleted). Its 13 virtual hooks became plain code.
   - `Options` + `GoalOptions` are now one `GoalOptions`.
   - `lib/device/` moved into `src/goal/`, since there is one image. `keyPin()` and `sleepHardware()` are gone.
   - `include/goal/board.h` is now `include/board.h`.
   - `buzzer_sounds.h` is folded into `sound.h`. `battery.h` and the strap check are in `telemetry.h`.
   - `[c6]` is folded into `[env:xiao_c6]`.
4. **Dead code:** the secrets.h compatibility shims for pre-auth and pre-telemetry files.
5. **Docs:**
   - README is rewritten goal-only (655 → 223 lines).
   - FLASHING loses §6 Scoreboard and *Scoreboard sound option*. §1–5 are unchanged, so ASSEMBLY's §1–5 links still work.
   - .gitignore is trimmed.
6. **Tools:**
   - mock_server is goal-only (549 → 283 lines): auth, config, status, goal and undo events, score per table, OTLP.
   - check_openapi runs a goal session: config, 201/409 goals, undo 201/409/already_undone/not_yours/too_late/closed, status, OTLP and auth.
   - The scoreboard schemas stay in openapi.yaml for backend; check_openapi says they no longer have a firmware producer.

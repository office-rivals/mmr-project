# Twin: plan C and the fastest ball (2026-10-18)

Asked by lead (reviewers' R2-B3): model plan C's chopped reads and check that the fastest ball counts at random
phases with radio and OLED activity, and that a bright-sun step recovers goals once plan C is on.

**Result:** new scenario `plan_c`; `drawer_light`'s sun step now counts mode a's goal (plan C on at the first
check). run-ci 19/19 at 31 and 36 mm (mock).

## Model (twin/src/devices.h, device.h)

- `beamChop()` = node with the LED off minus at 1 mA, both pull-ups, a point sample (the 2 × 0.25 ms settles
  aren't modelled).
- The app task can be blocked by the glue: a request start costs 2 ms (radio burst, EST); an OLED frame costs
  48 ms (EST: 2 lines × 16 cells × 16 bytes over 100 kHz I²C, U8x8 `drawString` in the app's tick, plus
  addressing). Only `plan_c` turns the OLED time on, because of the two issues below.

## Results (60 balls of 9 ms each, random phase, sun 2 × b's limit, chopped reads on)

| Condition | Missed |
|---|---|
| chop 3 ms, radio busy, screen off | 0 / 60 |
| chop 10 ms (the old value; mutation) | 8 / 60 |
| chop 3 ms, screen on (redraw on each goal and every 2 s) | 11–13 / 60 |

## Findings for firmware

1. **An OLED redraw blinds plan C.** `oled()` runs synchronously in the app's tick, so no chopped read is
   taken for the frame's I²C time. A 9 ms ball fits inside a 48 ms gap. The screen is on after a power-on or
   key wake, and for 10 s after any button release. Options: no redraw while chopping (or only line-by-line
   between polls), or draw only the changed cells.
2. **The ball level is read late.** `ball_ua` comes from `beamMv()` when the app runs, not at the D8 edge.
   Behind any stall (here an OLED frame) the ball has left: the read is the lit node (15 µA in a) and health
   says `ambient_high` in the dark. Seen in `drawer_queue` and `beam_faults` with the OLED time on. Options: take the
   reading only while the beam still reads blocked, or drop it when it was taken after the clear edge.

Both are KNOWN notes in `plan_c` for now; they become checks (and the OLED time the default) once fixed.

The EST timings want a bench check: scope a GPIO around `panel.drawString` and around a POST.

## Update (same day): both fixed by firmware

- `oled()` is non-blocking by contract (the glue's own draw task, below the app). The twin's OLED time is gone;
  `plan_c` now checks 0/60 misses with the screen on too.
- The in-ball light is read only while D8 still sees the ball, never in chopped mode. `plan_c` stalls the core
  100 ms at a ball (the goal is counted at 0.4 s, after the ball left) and checks that no `ambient_high` follows.
  For this the twin now queues beam and button edges like the glue (ISR time, delivered in the app's next loop).

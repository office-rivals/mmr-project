# Scoreboard UI spec

Owner: `ux`. Firmware builds exactly this; propose changes to ux. Mockups: `out/*.png` (contact
sheet `out/all.png`), generated by `uv run mockups.py`. Its `LAYOUT` block holds the pixel numbers
(firmware's `tools/make_fonts.py` imports it); the script fails if any text overflows its box.

**Panel:** 4.2" black/white e-paper, 400 × 300 px, 1-bit (user's final decision). 4 keys under the
screen at 20.32 mm (96 px) pitch, centred. A colour-TFT variant was designed and dropped; it is
kept for reference in `colour-v2/`.

## The whole UI in four rules

1. **The big numbers are the score. Your team's number is on your side.** The left half shows the
   team standing on the viewer's left. There is one board per table, at one end, facing along the
   table, so everyone looks at it from the same direction and "left" means the same to all players.
2. **A key does what is written right above it. No box = the key does nothing.**
3. **Nothing irreversible happens on one press.** Undo and an early "New game" ask first.
4. **When the board can't talk to the server, the score turns hollow and the keys disappear.**

## Layout

| Zone | px (y) | Share | Contents |
|------|--------|-------|----------|
| Status strip | 0–30 | top 10 % | Usually empty. A state word on the left ("Last game", "No connection"), icons on the right (Wi-Fi, battery). Turns black for "GOAL!" and "Not sent". |
| Score | 30–248 | middle | Two halves split by a vertical line. Score digits up to ~150 px (~30 mm) tall, readable at 3 m and more. Player names under the digits (20 px, ~4 mm). |
| Key labels | 248–298 | bottom 17 % | Four zones of 96 px (= the 20.32 mm key pitch), each centred over its key (x = 56/152/248/344). A label is a rounded box with 1–2 short lines. No label = no box. |

Font: DejaVu Sans Bold (free). Sizes: digits ≤ 170 (shrunk to fit "10"), heading 34, labels 20,
names 20, status 18, minimum 16 anywhere. Every text box keeps 2 px spare because GFX fonts can't
kern. Names that don't fit get an ellipsis ("Christopher-L…").

### Auto-submit: "Saving to MMR in 8 s" (user request 2026-10-15)

A linked match (players known) that is won on score is rated automatically
`Hardware:AutoSubmitDelaySeconds` (default 10) after the winning goal. Until then one Undo cancels it.

| # | Screen | When | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 |
|---|--------|------|----|----|----|----|
| 12d | **Finished, saving** | `match.submit_in_s != null`. Finished as usual (WINNER, summary line), plus "Saving to MMR in 8 s" centred in the label row over the empty K2/K3. **Only Undo is offered**: no Rematch and no QR until it is saved, so the one decision is "was that last goal real?". | ↶ Undo | – | – | – |
| 10b | **Undo? (while saving)** | K1 on 12d. Screen 10 plus "Saving in 4 s" in the same place. If the time runs out first, the screen closes into 12c (saved). | Cancel | – | – | ↶ Undo |
| 12c | **Finished, saved** | `match.needs_claim == false`: the match is rated (auto-submitted, confirmed or claimed). "Saved to MMR ✓" sits where the countdown was. No Undo (the server sends `can_undo: false`); a wrong scorer is fixed in the app. K4 **Rematch** (or New game). | – | – | – | Rematch / New game |

- **Undo confirmed** → back to Live at 9 : 7, and nothing is submitted (server). The countdown starts
  again at the next winning goal.
- **Countdown steps** (e-paper refreshes): the number rounds **up** to a 2 s step from 10 s down
  (10, 8, 6, 4, 2) and to a 10 s step above that (if the delay is configured longer). It is redrawn
  only when it changes, as a partial refresh of the label row: 5 refreshes for the default 10 s.
  The board counts down locally from the moment it received `submit_in_s`, and resyncs on every
  state.
- **The who-scored window for the winning goal** (2v2) must leave time to undo. It ends at a pick
  (+3 s, as on 09) or when **5 s are left** on the countdown, whichever comes first. Then 12d shows
  with ≥ 5 s for Undo. Once closed this way, the window stays closed (it doesn't reopen for the
  rest of its 15 s after the save). An unpicked winning goal stays unset and is fixed in the app. In 1v1 there is no who-scored window, so 12d shows at once
  with the full 10 s.
- **Undo races the save** (the submit lands first; the server answers 422): the strip shows
  **"Already saved. Fix it in the app."** (inverted, 3 s, 13b) instead of "Not sent", then 12c.
- **No connection** during the countdown: 14 as usual (hollow digits, no keys). The server still
  submits on time; when the board is back it shows 12c.
- A game ended with **Save** on "End this game?" is not won on score, so it is not auto-submitted:
  Finished with the claim QR, as before.

With 1v1 (each team has one player) the "who scored?" screen is skipped: the scorer is obvious.
In 1v1, an own goal is fixed in the app. Team size always comes from the roster the server sends.
Nothing is hard-coded.

## Scan-in: badges, teams and LEDs (user request 2026-10-15)

Players tap their badge on the **scan target** (the badge mark on the face, in the wing to the right of the
screen; v5), the board shows who is in, and one key makes the teams: chosen, random or fair. Playing without badges still
works exactly as before: the first goal starts an unlinked game, and it is claimed with the QR.
Server side: backend's `api/docs/hardware-roster-proposal.md` (`state.roster`, actions `scan`,
`remove_player`, `generate_teams {mode}`).

### Flow

```
 Idle(01) ─key/motion─► Lobby(18) ─badge─► "✓ Anna joined" … ─4th badge─► All in(18d) ─K2/K3/K4─► Teams(19)
  "Press any key, then      │                                                                     │ first goal
   tap your badge →"        └─ unknown badge ─► Lobby + QR (21) ─ linked in the app ─► "Anna" in   ▼
                                                                                              Live(04) … Finished(12)
 All in(18d) ─K2 Choose─► Who plays with Alice?(23) ─name─► Teams(19d)   (8 s, no pick ─► back to 18d)
 Finished(12) ─K4 Rematch─► Teams(19), same teams          Finished(12) ─badge (first 60 s)─► new Lobby
```

1. **A badge can't wake the board.** Idle (01b) says "Press a key, then tap your badge →" in the
   empty label row; the arrow points at the scan target in the wing right of the screen (`BADGE_ARROW`:
   ← on a mirrored build). Table motion wakes it too.
2. **Lobby (18)** replaces Ready when no teams are linked: strip "Who's playing?", the heading "Tap
   your badge →", one slot per player (2 × the league's team size, so 4 in 2v2 and 2 in 1v1) filled
   in join order, and "2 more to start". K1 **Remove player** appears once someone is in.
3. **Tapping:** while the reader listens, the target light gives a soft blink every 3 s
   ("you can scan now"). On touch, the board blips and the target lights white (the card is read,
   keep it there for a moment). **Every tap of ≥ 0.3 s counts**, on any scan-accepting screen and at
   any moment, including while the e-paper redraws (firmware keeps polling the reader during
   refreshes). The screen may lag; the light never does. **White light + blip are the receipt**
   (the backup if a tap is still missed): no white light means it wasn't read, so tap again. A
   receipt always ends in an answer: the chirp + "✓ Anna joined", the low note + its toast, or
   "✗ Not sent. Tap again." if the server doesn't confirm within 5 s. A scan is never held back by
   a key action that is still waiting (a tap is a fact; the server ignores `expected_version` for
   it): it is sent at once, or right after that request. They come straight from
   the reader, never wait for the e-paper, and never show for a read that will be dropped. The
   player is added when the badge is pulled away (firmware filters
   fixed tags that way, ~0.3 s after release). Then the strip shows "✓ Anna joined" (3 s), the
   target glows in Anna's colour for 1 s, and her colour lights above her slot's key. A second tap of the same badge shows "Anna is already in" (no
   toggle: a double tap must never remove anyone).
4. **All in (18d):** "All in!" and three ways to make the teams (user, 2026-10-15), shown while
   `roster.can_generate`. Teams are not made automatically, so a wrong join can still be fixed first
   (K1 Remove player stays).
   - **K4 Fair**: balanced by MMR (`generate_teams {mode: fair}`, as before; far right, where "Make
     teams" was).
   - **K3 Random**: a random split (`mode: random`).
   - **K2 Choose**: opens **Who plays with Alice? (23)**, Alice being the first to join. K2–K4 carry
     the other three names in join order, their LEDs in those players' colours. One press makes both
     teams (in 2v2 Alice's partner decides everything): Alice and the pick play on the left, the other
     two on the right (`mode: manual`). Nothing pressed for 8 s → back to 18d, nothing made (strip
     "Choose teams (or wait)", as Remove who?). K1 is empty: there is nothing to cancel. Why not
     "each key toggles a side": that needs a Done key and allows 3 v 1; the badge target can't tell
     sides; join order would make people plan before scanning.
5. **Teams (19):** Ready with names; the strip names how they were made, from `match.teams_mode`:
   "Fair teams" (19), "Random teams" (19c), "Teams chosen" (19d), or "Teams made" when null (linked
   in the app) and always in 1v1. The left half is the team that plays at the viewer's left end, as
   everywhere. Keys: K1 **Remove player**, K2 **⇄ Swap sides** (both teams change ends), K3
   **Again** only on random teams (19c): a new draw with a different pairing, as often as they
   like (the same key as Random; a re-draw gives nothing away that Choose doesn't). K4 is empty.
   The first goal locks the teams and starts Live. For a different mode, remove a player and
   rejoin, or use the app (no "Other teams" key, lead).
6. **Remove player** (on 18 or 19) opens **Remove who? (20)**: the keys carry "✗ name" (on 19 in
   team order, left team K1–K2 and right team K3–K4; on 18 in join order) and the LEDs light in
   those players' colours. One press removes that player; nothing happens after 8 s (the strip says
   "Remove who? (or wait)"). Removing from Teams dissolves the teams and returns to the Lobby. A
   wrong removal is undone by tapping the badge again.
7. **Unknown badge (21):** the lobby stays, the names move up, and the QR of `roster.link.url`
   (`/t/{code}`, same QR rules as screen 12) appears with "Link this badge:" / "you or any
   colleague can scan". Whoever scans it (any signed-in colleague, user decision) links the badge
   to themselves or picks the player it belongs to, so a helper can set up someone who isn't signed
   in on their phone. That player joins at once (the chirp doesn't play: nobody tapped), and the QR
   goes away. It also goes away when the link expires (10 min), when another unknown badge replaces it,
   or when teams are made. K1 and K4 keep working.
   **One QR at a time: the screen decides.** The Lobby (18/21) shows only `roster.link`; Idle,
   Waking and Finished (01/02/12) show only `claim_url`. A badge tapped on Finished opens the
   Lobby, so the link wins from then on; the finished match can still be claimed from the table
   page (side NFC sticker).
8. **1v1** (league team size 1): 2 slots. All in shows only K4 **Make teams** (18e): there is one
   possible split, so no modes (sends `mode: fair`; the server puts them at ends as it likes, and
   Swap sides fixes it). Teams shows the 1v1 layout (19b, "Teams made", no Again), Remove who? uses
   K1 and K4.
9. **After the game:** on Finished, K4 reads **Rematch** when the game had players: same teams,
   back to Teams (19) at 0 : 0, where they can swap or change. A badge tapped on Finished starts a
   new lobby with that player instead, but only in the **first 60 s** that Finished is shown (the
   next group is usually waiting right there). Each wake onto Finished starts a new 60 s window, so
   later arrivals follow the Idle hint: press any key, then tap. Without players, K4 stays "New
   game" (→ Lobby).
10. **Discard** on "End this game?" with a linked roster returns to Teams (19), not to an empty lobby.

**Toasts** (strip, 3 s, from the `scan` result):

| `result` | Strip |
|----------|-------|
| `joined` | ✓ Anna joined |
| `already_in` | Anna is already in |
| `unknown_card` | (none: the QR screen 21 says it, from `roster.link`) |
| `roster_full` | Full. Remove a player first. (22) |
| `busy` | Anna is in another game |
| `locked` | Game on. Scan when it's over. |
| `no_league` | This table has no league |
| `unavailable` | Badges are switched off |
| phone (firmware: a 4-byte UID starting 0x08 is a random phone ID, not sent) | Phone? Tap the left side. (`PHONE_TOAST`) |

**Where the reader listens** (polling only while one of these shows, for power): Lobby, Teams,
Remove who?, Who plays with?, and **Finished for its first 60 s** (restarted on every wake onto Finished, not by key
presses). It is off on Live, confirm screens, who-scored, Finished after 60 s, and while asleep. The same badge again within 3 s is ignored (it
rests on the target). Fixed tags (the table's own NTAG stickers) are ignored by firmware.

**Timeouts:**

| What | Value | Where |
|------|-------|-------|
| Join toast | 3 s | board |
| Same badge again | ignored for 3 s | board |
| Remove who? | 8 s → back, nothing removed | board |
| Who plays with? (23) | 8 s → back to All in, no teams made | board |
| Reader on Finished | first 60 s after it appears or the board wakes onto it | board |
| Board stays awake while the lobby has players | 5 min after the last join, then normal sleep (the lobby survives on the server; a key press shows it again) | board |
| Card link QR | until used, replaced, or 10 min | server (`roster.link.expires_in_s`) |
| Lobby with no activity | cleared after 30 min | server |
| Teams made, no goal | the existing linked-match timeout | server |

### LEDs: off unless they say something

One RGB LED above each key, behind a glossy opal bar (four segments), plus one **target light** under the badge target (v5: in the wing right of the screen)
(user, 2026-10-15; no extra strip LEDs). **Rule: a steady key LED means "this key is this player";
a flash is feedback; the target light is lit only while scanning works.** They never show anything the screen doesn't, so a player who can't see
colour misses nothing. Electronics' battery budget (scoreboard battery life: INTERFACES §0) allows only a few minutes of light a
day, so steady light is short and always times out.

| Screen / event | LEDs |
|----------------|------|
| **Target light:** reader listening (Lobby, Teams, Remove who?, first 60 s of Finished) | a soft white blink every 3 s (0.3 s fade up and down, dim). Off the moment the reader stops, so it never promises a scan that won't work |
| **Target light:** badge touches it | white, bright, while the badge is there (max 2 s), instant before the server answers |
| **Target light:** `joined` / `already_in` | that player's colour, steady 1 s, then back to blinking |
| **Target light:** unknown, full, busy, phone, errors | off for 1 s, then back to blinking (the screen says what happened; red is a player colour, so no "error red") |
| Key LEDs on a scan | nothing extra: in the Lobby, the new player's colour simply appears above their slot's key (row below) |
| Lobby (18) | steady: joined players' colours in join order (K1 = first in), for **10 s** after the last join, then off |
| Teams (19) | steady: each player's colour above "their" key (left team K1–K2, right K3–K4, in the order of the names; 1v1: K1 and K4), for **10 s** after the teams appear or change, then off. These are the keys they will press on "Who scored?" |
| Remove who? (20), Who plays with? (23) | steady: each name key in that player's colour, until a pick or 8 s |
| Goal | the scoring side's two LEDs blink twice (~1 s) in their players' colours (white without players), with the Goal sound |
| Won | the winning side's LEDs blink 4 times with the fanfare (~1 s) |
| Any accepted key press | that key's LED white, 0.2 s (the board took it; the label inverts next) |
| Idle, Waking, Live, who-scored, confirms, Finished, No connection, Not set up | key LEDs off; target light off (except the first 60 s of Finished, see above) |
| Low battery (15) | flashes only: no steady key light and no listening blink (touch and join feedback stay). Change batteries (16): all off |

- **Budget:** about 1 min of light per game (two 10 s steady phases, ~17 goal flashes, a few scans
  and presses), so ~10 min a day at the §3 usage. The target's listening blink is a ~10 % duty
  cycle over ~3 min of listening per game, so it adds ~20 s of light per game; the PCA9685 sleeps
  between blinks. Electronics confirms; the two 10 s values are
  firmware constants if the numbers are tight.
- **Brightness:** one fixed level (firmware constant, set on the bench): clearly visible under
  office light from 1.5 m, never glaring. White-balanced so the six colours look equally bright. No
  remote setting (add one like `sound_volume` only if asked).
- The PCA9685 sleeps whenever no LED is lit.

**Colours: 6, not 8.** On a diffused 5 mm RGB LED behind a light pipe at ~2 mA, only the primaries
and secondaries are safe to tell apart: **red, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta** (index 0–5).
Orange sits between red and yellow, and purple/pink between blue and magenta; side by side at low
brightness (and for the ~8 % of men with red-green colour blindness) they read as the same. White
is reserved for feedback flashes and for "no players". The app shows the same six hues, so a
player's colour is the same everywhere. With six, clashes in a 4-player roster are more common, but
backend's "next free index" rule handles that. The e-paper shows no colour (B/W); the colour lives
on the LEDs and in the app.

## Transitions and timeouts

```
            key / motion                  server state
 Idle(01) ─────────────► Waking(02) ─────────────────► Ready(03) / Live(04) / Finished(12)
    ▲                       │ >10 s no server                    │
    │ table idle            ▼                                     │ goal, a team has ≥ 2 players
    │ (idle_timeout_s)   No connection(14) ── server back ──►     ▼
    └────────────────── any screen              Goal(08) ── name ──► Picked(09) ── 3 s ──► Live
                                                   └──── 15 s, no press (scorer stays unset) ──► Live
 Live ─K1─► Undo?(10) ── K4 ──► Live (score −1)        Live ─K4─► End game?(11) ── K1 Save ──► Finished(12, QR) / K2 Discard ──► Ready
               └─ K1 (Cancel) / 8 s ──► Live                        └─ K4 (Cancel) / 8 s ──► Live
```

| Timeout | Value | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Who-scored window | 15 s from the goal | Counted on the server (`last_goal` age), so a reboot or reconnect doesn't restart it. Afterwards the scorer stays unset and can be fixed in the app. |
| Picked → Live | 3 s | Correction window. |
| Confirm screens (10, 11) | 8 s → Cancel | |
| Press not confirmed | 5 s → Not sent (13) | |
| Not-sent strip | 3 s | |
| No connection | after 10 s without the server | Retries with backoff (firmware); any key retries at once. |
| Sleep | `idle_timeout_s` (server config) | Before sleeping, redraw as Idle (01) with a full refresh, so the image left on the panel is clean. |

## Wrong and odd presses

| Situation | Behaviour | Why |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Key with no label | Nothing happens. No inversion, no request. | Rule 2: no box means nothing. |
| Any key, any screen | It does exactly what the screen currently shows above it. Screen and keys come from the same state, never two. | Rule 2. A label showing Undo while the key picks a scorer is a bug. |
| Any key while Idle, Waking, No connection or Not set up | Wakes the board or retries now. It never runs the action whose old label might still be visible. | The image may be stale after sleep. |
| Same key twice in a row (bounce, nervous double-tap) | The 2nd press is ignored if it comes < 0.5 s after the screen changed. On 10 and 11, the key you just pressed is **Cancel**. | A double press can never confirm. |
| An unlabelled key on 10/11 | Nothing (rule 2). Only Cancel or the 8 s timeout leaves without acting. | One rule everywhere; the timeout is the safe default. |
| Two keys at once | Ignored, except K1 + any other key, which reads as K1 (K1 shorts the ladder). Firmware accepts a key only if the reading stays in one key's window for the whole press. | A wide finger must not trigger a third key's action. **Constraint:** K1 is never a one-press destructive action. Today it is Undo (it only opens a confirm), Cancel, Save, a scorer name (correctable for 3 s), Remove player (opens a picker), or a name on Remove who? (undone by tapping that badge again), so K1 winning is always the safe outcome. |
| A press while a request is still waiting for its answer | Ignored. A badge tap is the exception: it is sent at once or right after (see Scan-in, Flow 3). | One action at a time, no queue. A tap already got its receipt, so it must not vanish. |
| Board and app act at once (if the app gets the same actions later) | The server takes the first. The board shows the server's new state. A rejected second action (409) shows nothing; the board just redraws. | |
| A goal arrives while 10/11 is open | The confirm screen stays open. The score under it updates. Undo removes whatever goal is last when you confirm (the server decides). | |
| A new goal while the who-scored screen is open | Who-scored restarts for the new goal. The previous goal's scorer stays unset. | |

## Battery swap and boot (power can vanish at any instant)

- The panel keeps whatever it showed when the power went, possibly a half-finished screen. The
  board stores **no** UI state: on boot it shows whatever the server says.
- **Cold boot (cells inserted):** don't touch the panel until the server answers. Then do **one
  full refresh** straight to the right screen. That is a single flash, and no "booting" screen
  appears in between. If there's no answer within **5 s**, do one full refresh to Waking (02),
  drawn over the last-known score if cached, else 0 : 0, with no labels. Then follow the normal
  rules (14 after 10 s).
- **Wake from deep sleep:** the firmware knows what's on the panel (it drew Idle), so it uses one
  partial refresh for the Waking icon, then one more for the answer.
- Presses during boot are ignored. The labels on screen may belong to the previous life.
- **Unconfirmed press:** the label stays inverted until the server answers. A press with no answer
  within 5 s → Not sent (13). The press is **dropped, never replayed** (no queue survives a reboot
  or a reconnect). Players press again if they still want it, because replaying an "Undo"
  later would be wrong.

## Refresh rules (e-paper)

- Redraw only on change. Partial refresh for labels, the strip and score changes. A full refresh
  (~2 s, visible flash) at the start of each game, when going to sleep, on cold boot, and after
  every 20 partial refreshes. Never do a full refresh in the 15 s after a goal (people are
  looking).
- **With the badge reader:** while the reader listens, full refreshes wait (partials carry on),
  unless the bench shows the reader works through one (`RFID_DURING_FULL`). Two exceptions keep
  the screen clean without surprising anyone:
  - **Making teams** (Fair, Random, a pick on 23, or 1v1 Make teams: All in → Teams) is the start-of-game full refresh. **Again** is a partial (only names change). The reader pauses for those
    ~2 s, and the target light stops blinking, so it doesn't promise a scan. Nobody needs to scan
    then.
  - A **deferred** full refresh runs at the next moment the reader is off *and* it isn't within
    15 s of a goal, or at the sleep redraw, whichever comes first. It must never land right after the
    first goal (Teams → Live stops the reader, but people are looking then).
- Hollow digits (14) use a 3 px outline, so "stale" is visible without colour or words.

## Sound (user request 2026-10-15)

> **What the sounds sound like is in `SOUNDS-V2.md`** (user decision 2026-10-15). The build is a
> mini speaker playing samples, with the buzzer as a fallback build, and both have note tables
> there. The sounds are the same seven: these six plus **Saved**, which plays when the 12d
> countdown ends in a save. This section keeps **when** each sound plays, plus the rules and the
> volume. The "Notes" column below is the old v1 buzzer table, kept for reference only. Rule 6's
> octave constant applies to the buzzer build only.

Three game sounds plus badge sounds: **up = goal, down = goal taken back, fanfare = game won; blip = badge read, chirp = you're in, low note = not joined.** Everything a
sound says is also on the screen, so a muted board or a player who can't hear misses nothing.

| Sound | When | Notes (Hz) and durations | Length |
|-------|------|--------------------------|--------|
| **Goal** | A new goal reaches the board. | G6 1568 · 60 ms, gap 20, C7 2093 · 140 ms (a rising "ba-ding") | 220 ms |
| **Won** | The new goal ends the game (`game` is `finished` in the same state as the new `last_goal`). It plays **instead of** Goal, at once; it doesn't wait for screen 12. | C6 1047 · 100, gap 20, E6 1319 · 100, gap 20, G6 1568 · 100, gap 20, C7 2093 · 400 | 760 ms |
| **Undo** | The server confirms an `undo` this board sent (after K4 on screen 10). | G6 1568 · 100 ms, gap 30, C6 1047 · 180 ms (falling, the Goal sound backwards and slower) | 310 ms |
| **Scan** | A badge touches the scan target (local, instant, with the white LED flash). Not repeated for the same badge within 3 s. Kept: it is the tap's receipt, and the server's answer comes ≥ 0.5 s later, so it never blurs with the chirp. | C7 2093 · 50 ms (one short "bip", like any badge reader) | 50 ms |
| **Joined** | The server answers `joined` or `already_in` (either way: you're in). Tap, hear it, walk away. | C7 2093 · 40, gap 10, E7 2637 · 40, gap 10, G7 3136 · 80 (a quick rising "bi-di-ding": three fast notes, higher and shorter than the Goal "ba-ding") | 180 ms |
| **Not joined** | Any other `scan` result: `unknown_card`, `roster_full`, `busy`, `locked`, `no_league`, `unavailable`, a phone, or the scan not confirmed (Not sent). Soft, never a harsh buzz: the screen explains. | E6 1319 · 120 ms (one low, gentle note) | 120 ms |

Silent on purpose:

| Event | Why no sound |
|-------|--------------|
| Key presses, scorer picked, swap sides, confirm screens | The switch clicks already; a beep would double it. |
| New game, Save, Discard | The person pressing is looking at the board. |
| Waking (motion or key), Ready | Motion wakes the board whenever someone walks past. |
| Low battery, Change batteries | A repeating chirp is the smoke-alarm sound everyone hates. The screen says it. |
| Not sent, No connection, Not set up | The presser is looking at the board, and an error beep makes it sound broken. |
| A game ended with Save (not by a goal) | Someone chose to stop; no fanfare. |

**Rules that keep it from getting annoying:**

1. **Sound only for what just happened.** Play Goal/Won only if `last_goal.id` is new to this board
   *and* `last_goal.age_s ≤ 3`. A goal the board learns about later (reconnect, wake, boot, slow
   network) stays silent: a beep 10 s after the ball went in confuses more than it helps. Undo
   sounds only on the server's answer to this board's own request, never on a state change the
   board merely notices (so a Discard to 0 : 0 can't sound like an Undo).
2. **Each goal sounds once.** The last sounded goal id lives in RAM. After a reboot it is empty,
   and rule 1's 3 s age limit covers the gap (a reboot plus Wi-Fi takes longer than 3 s).
3. **Never in Idle, Waking, No connection, Not set up, Change batteries, or during boot.**
4. **A new sound cuts off the one still playing.** No queue, no overlap.
5. **Sound never delays the screen or a key.** The player runs in the background (firmware).
6. **Volume comes from the server** (below). The socketed series resistor sets the maximum: High
   is clearly audible to the far players (~1.5 m) over play, not across the office. Firmware has
   one constant that shifts all three sounds by whole octaves, so
   they can sit near the buzzer's loudest frequency without changing their shape.

**Volume and mute: one server setting per table, nothing on the board** (user, 2026-10-15).

- The table has `sound_volume`: **0 = Off, 1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High; default 2.** The device
  config carries it as `sound_volume`; a missing field means 2. It is pulled with the config on
  every wake, so a change applies by the next wake at the latest.
- Firmware maps 1–3 to PWM duty, calibrated on the bench so each step is clearly louder than the
  one below (Low still audible at ~1.5 m in a quiet room). 0 plays nothing at all.
- Admin UI: one four-way control, **"Scoreboard sound: Off / Low / Medium / High"**, on the
  table's settings. Words, not a slider or numbers.
- **No key combo.** A hidden combo breaks rule 2 (a key does what is written above it). Someone
  would mute it by accident or as a joke, and nobody at the table would know how to undo it. The
  admin control is the only one, and it is easy to find.
- No quiet hours and no volume or mute icon on the screen: the board only makes sound when people are
  at the table doing something (scoring, undoing, winning, tapping a badge), never on its own, and
  silence never needs explaining because the screen says everything.

## What the server must send (input for `firmware/openapi.yaml`)

Already ordered for **this** board (the server knows which end of the table the board sits at):
`left`/`right` = `{score, players: [names], end_label}`; `game`: `none | live | finished`; `winner`:
`left | right | null`; `last_goal`: `{id, side, scorer | null, age_s}`; `can_undo`; and a version
for long-poll. Actions: `undo`, `new_game {save: true|false}` (when finished: just start one),
`claim_url` (the short `/t/{code}` link, target ≤ ~60 chars; non-null exactly while the shown match is finished and unclaimed/unconfirmed) for the after-game QR. `assign_scorer {goal_id, player}`, `swap_sides` (only while the score is 0 : 0 and a roster is
linked; the server says so with `can_swap`). Who-scored is shown when `last_goal.scorer == null`,
`age_s < 15`, and either team has ≥ 2 players (as screen 08; an own goal in 2v1 is possible).
Scan-in (backend's proposal, with the differences listed in the report to lead): `roster` = `{league_name,
team_size, players: [{id, name, color}], can_generate, link: {url, expires_in_s} | null} | null`;
`match.submit_in_s`: seconds until the auto-submit, or null (none pending). While it is set, the board shows 12d and hides the QR even if `claim_url` is set. Rated = `match.needs_claim == false` on Finished (12c, "Saved to MMR ✓"). Not `claim_url == null`: that is also null in deployments without a public frontend URL (backend). `submit_in_s` is only ever set while `needs_claim` is true.
`match.summary`: `{duration_s | null, mvp_name | null}`, on finished matches only, for the Finished line. No reason field is needed: a missing MVP just shows no MVP.
`left`/`right` players gain `id` and `color` (0–5). Actions `scan {uid}` (response `scan.result`),
`remove_player {player_id}`, `generate_teams {mode, teams}`:
- `mode`: `fair | random | manual`, optional, default `fair` (backend's names; older boards keep working).
- `teams` (manual only): `[[left ids], [right ids]]`, as the board shows them, team_size each; 422 on
  wrong sizes, an id not on the roster, or a duplicate. The board sends `[[first joined, pick], [the other two]]`.
- `random` is also accepted on random teams at 0 : 0 (Again): it replaces the teams with a
  different pairing (2v2: one of the other two), ends at random. On fair or manual teams it is a 422
  (the board never offers Again there).
- `match.teams_mode`: `fair | random | manual | null` (null = linked in the app), kept by a rematch
  and by Discard back to Teams, for the Teams strip and the Again key.

`new_game` on a finished game with players =
rematch with the same teams; Discard with a linked roster returns to Teams. The board shows "Remove
player" while `roster` is non-null with players, or on Teams before the first goal. `clear_roster`
is not used (v1).

Config (`GET /devices/{id}/config`): `sound_volume` (int 0–3, 0 = off, 2 when missing), from the
table's `sound_volume`. The Undo sound needs nothing new: it plays on the answer to the board's own `undo`.

## Decisions and why (one line each)

| Decision | Why |
|----------|-----|
| No team names or colours, just left/right halves | Position is the label: "the number on my side" needs no reading, and it works in 1-bit. |
| Left half = the team on the viewer's left | One board at one end, facing along the table: every player sees it from the same side. The user may override this default. |
| Only 2 keys in use on Live | Fewer choices = no hesitation. The middle keys stay empty, so no "what does this do?" press. |
| Undo far left, New game far right | A slip lands on an empty key, never on the other action. |
| Who-scored reuses the Live screen | The score stays big after a goal. The names already sit over their own team's half, so no new screen to learn. |
| Own goal = pick an opponent's name | No "Own goal" key or sub-screen. The four names are the four people at the table. The strip then says "Oops! Own goal, Alice": it confirms the reading and keeps it light. |
| Auto-submit: only Undo during the countdown, the count in 2 s steps | One decision at a time ("was that goal real?"), and 5 e-paper refreshes instead of 10. Rematch and the QR wait until it is saved. |
| Winning-goal who-scored ends with 5 s left | Undo must always be reachable before the save; the scorer can still be set afterwards. |
| Finished summary on one line, over the half that lost | The strip there is empty (WINNER sits over the other half), so the score keeps its full size. MVP only with enough scorer data, so it's never a guess. |
| Ignoring who-scored is fine (15 s) | Play must never wait for the board. The scorer is optional data. |
| Who-scored skipped in 1v1 | The answer is obvious. Don't ask. |
| Confirm puts Cancel on the key you just pressed | A double press or bounce cancels instead of confirming. |
| New game asks Save / Discard / Cancel (user) | Save and Discard are side by side (K1, K2), away from Cancel (K4). |
| No confirm for New game when finished | Nothing is lost, so don't add a step. |
| QR only while `claim_url` is set, bottom centre | That's the moment someone wants to log who played. The bottom centre is free there (K2/K3 unused), and the digits keep almost their full size. |
| Save on "End this game?" goes to Finished, not Ready (backend: done) | The players can claim the saved match at once from the QR. |
| NFC first, QR as fallback, hint names both | Tapping beats scanning, but not every phone has NFC on. The QR still works from ~20–40 cm. |
| Swap sides only at 0 : 0, on K2, no confirm | Wrong sides show up the moment the names appear. Before any goal a swap loses nothing, and pressing it again undoes it. Later the fix belongs in the app, not on a key someone might hit mid-game. |
| K1 is always the safe key | The ladder reads K1 whenever K1 is part of a multi-key press. |
| Hollow digits when offline | It shows "maybe old" with no words. The score stays readable. |
| Labels vanish when offline/booting | A key without a label does nothing (rule 2), so no press is ever lost silently. |
| Unconfirmed press is dropped, never replayed | A late Undo or New game would hit the wrong goal or game. |
| Waking shows the old image + an icon, not a splash | No flicker, and the last score is still useful. |
| One full refresh on cold boot, straight to the real screen | A battery swap causes one flash, not three. |
| "Change batteries" full screen, then sleep | The board's last image tells the next person what to do, at zero power. |
| Not set up shows the device ID only | The admin needs exactly that, and there's no URL or secret on screen (public repo). |
| Badge: blip on read, chirp when in, one low note otherwise | Tap and walk away: the chirp says "you're in" without looking. Two outcomes, two sounds; the screen gives the reason. |
| Three sounds: goal up, undo down, win fanfare | Up/down needs no learning. Everything else stays silent, and nothing sounds that the screen doesn't also show. |
| Goals older than 3 s stay silent | A late beep after a reconnect or wake would confuse players, and replays never sound twice. |
| Volume (Off/Low/Medium/High) only in the admin UI, default Medium (user) | A hidden key combo breaks rule 2 and gets used as a prank. One control an admin can find is enough. |
| Badges join a lobby; one key makes teams (not automatic) | A wrong join can be removed before the teams exist. One press, clearly labelled. |
| Three team modes on All in: K2 Choose, K3 Random, K4 Fair (user) | Fair stays on K4, where "Make teams" was. 1v1 has one split, so it keeps the single key. |
| Choose = "Who plays with Alice?", one press | In 2v2 one partner fixes both teams: one screen, one press, no Done key, no 3 v 1. Swap sides then sets the ends. |
| Random can be re-drawn (Again) | It's for fun and variety; anyone who wants specific teams can Choose anyway, so re-draws cost nothing. |
| Remove = "Remove player", then the name | Names on keys are already known from "Who scored?". A wrong removal is fixed by tapping the badge again. |
| Second tap of the same badge does nothing | A double tap must never remove someone. |
| Rematch on Finished | Office games repeat the same four; rescanning every game would be a chore. A new group just taps a badge. |
| LEDs: steady = "this key is this player", flashes = feedback, both short | The battery budget (INTERFACES §0) allows only minutes of light a day. Colour is a bonus: the names are always on screen. |
| 6 LED colours, not 8 | Orange and purple can't be told apart from their neighbours on a diffused LED. |
| Words in English, ≤ 3 per label | The app is in English. Icons (↶, ✓, ✗, Wi-Fi, battery) are added only where nobody would misread them. |

## Hallway test (15 min, 3 colleagues who haven't seen this)

Print `out/04-live.png` … `16-change-batteries.png` at 100 % scale (85 mm wide). Tape each print
above four coins or keys at 20 mm pitch, prop it at 50° (v5) and stand people 1 m away. Ask, without
explaining anything first:

1. 04: "What's the score? Which one is your team?" (point to a side for them)
2. 04: "That last goal shouldn't count. Fix it." (expect K1, then K4 on 10)
3. 08: "Carol scored." / "Bob knocked it into his own goal."
3b. 12c: "How long did that game take? Who was best?" (check that "9:26" isn't read as a clock time)
4. 04: "You want to quit this game and not count it." (K4, then K2)
5. 14: "Is this score up to date? What would you do?"
6. 16 and 17: "What does this mean? Who do you call?"
7. 07 and 13: "You pressed Undo and this appeared. What happened?"
8. 01b: "You want to play with your badges. Go." (expect: press a key, then tap the target)
9. 18b: "Bob isn't playing after all. Fix it." (K1, then ✗ Bob)
10. 19: "You're standing at the wrong ends."
10b. 18d: "You want to play Alice with Carol." (K2 Choose, then Carol) / "Let the board decide, but evenly." (K4 Fair)

**Pass:** every person answers every task correctly without help, each within ~5 s. Nobody presses

table's level in the admin UI first, then the resistor (maximum) or the octave constant (pitch); change the sounds only if 2 of 3 dislike them.

# Scoreboard UI spec (v2: colour IPS TFT)

Owner: `ux`. Firmware builds exactly this; propose changes to ux. Mockups: `out/*.png`. Overview
sheets: `out/all.png` (every screen), `out/anim.png` (motion frames), `out/all-dim.png` and
`out/backlight.png` (rough look at idle backlight in a lit room, 45°). All are generated by
`uv run mockups.py`. Its `LAYOUT` and `COLOURS` blocks are the contract: firmware's
`tools/make_fonts.py` imports them. The script fails if any text overflows its box.

**Panel:** 2.8" IPS ST7789, 320 × 240 landscape (0.18 mm/px), electronics' preliminary pick. 4 keys
under the screen. Label zones are the screen's quarters (x = 40/120/200/280 px). The switches sit at
15.24 mm pitch (6 perfboard holes), within 1.3 mm of each label centre.

**Colour adds meaning on top of a layout that works without it.** Every state is still readable in
greyscale (hollow digits, missing labels, icons, words), because ~8 % of men are colour-blind and
a dim backlight washes colours out first (see `out/backlight.png`).

## The whole UI in four rules

1. **The big numbers are the score. Your team's number is on your side, under your colour.** The
   left half shows the team standing on the viewer's left. Each board faces along the table, so the
   two boards are mirror images of each other.
2. **A key does what is written right above it. No box = the key does nothing.**
3. **Nothing irreversible happens on one press.** Undo and an early "New game" ask first.
4. **When the board can't talk to the server, the score turns hollow and the keys disappear.**

## Layout and colour

| Zone | px (y) | Contents |
|------|--------|----------|
| Status strip | 0–24 | Usually empty and black. Icons on the right (Wi-Fi, battery). It fills with a colour for an event: the scoring team's colour for "GOAL! Who scored?", red for "✗ Not sent". |
| Countdown | 24–27 | Only on who-scored: a white bar that shrinks to nothing over the 15 s window. |
| Team bar | 28–32 | A bar in the team's colour across the top of each half. |
| Score | 34–178 | Two halves split by a grey line. White digits. Single digits are ~115 px (~20 mm) tall; "10" is smaller so it fits. Names (18 px, ~3 mm) under the digits, in the team's colour. |
| Key labels | 186–238 | Four 80 px zones, one per key. A rounded box with an outline and 1–2 short lines. No label = no box. |

| Colour | Hex (RGB565-exact) | Means | Used for |
|--------|--------------------|-------|----------|
| Black | `000000` | background | everything; the least light, the most contrast |
| White | `FFFFFF` | the facts | digits, labels, headings |
| Team left / right | `F85858` / `50A4F8` (defaults) | which team | team bar, names, GOAL strip, name-key outlines, winner half (`481818` / `183048`) |
| Green | `38DC78` | done / keep | picked scorer, Save |
| Red | `F84848` | failed / lose it | Not sent, Discard, Change batteries |
| Amber | `F8BC00` | attention | No connection, low battery, Not set up |
| Grey | `585858` | nothing | the divider only, never information |

- **Team colours come from the server** (per table: the colour of each side's rods), with the
  defaults above. They are the only team identity. No team names.
- Red appears both as a team default and as "failed". They never share a zone: team colour stays in
  the score area, the label outlines and the GOAL strip, and red-as-error only appears with a ✗ or
  in a confirm label. If the table's rods are red, keep it like this.
- Fonts: DejaVu Sans Bold, 1-bit glyphs (Adafruit GFX), with 2 px spare per box because GFX can't
  kern. Sizes: digits ≤ 160 (shrunk to fit), heading 28, names 18, labels 17, status 16, minimum 15.

## Backlight

The backlight is the biggest power draw, so its level is part of the UI.

| When | Level | Why |
|------|-------|-----|
| A match is running, nothing is happening | **idle, ≥ 20 %** (`BACKLIGHT_PCT.idle`) | See `out/backlight.png`. At 5 % the names and labels disappear in a lit room and the digits are grey. At 10 % the digits are readable but the names are borderline. 20 % is the lowest level where everything on screen can still be read at 45°. |
| Goal, key press, any change from the server, wake | **boost 100 % for 5 s**, then a 400 ms fade back to idle | Draws the eye to the change; the fade makes it calm instead of a blink. |
| Who-scored window | boost at the goal only (not held for all 15 s) | Power. The labels are readable at idle. |
| Between matches | **off, panel asleep (black)** | Power. The last score isn't shown, unlike e-paper. |

Electronics owns the budget. If 20 % idle can't fit, tell ux before going lower: below 20 %,
names and labels must switch to white (the team bars keep the colour).

## Screens

Live-score keys are fixed: **K1 = ↶ Undo, K4 = New game, K2/K3 unused.** Undo and New game sit at
opposite ends so a slip can't hit the wrong one.

| # | Screen | When | K1 | K2 | K3 | K4 |
|---|--------|------|----|----|----|----|
| 01 | **Waking (cached)** | Wake from deep sleep. The last score from RTC memory, **hollow** (= not confirmed yet), with the "connecting" dots. No labels. | – | – | – | – |
| 02 | **Waking (cold)** | After a battery swap there's no cache: black with only the connecting dots. | – | – | – | – |
| 03 | **Ready** | Awake, no goals in this game yet. Shows 0 : 0 and the names. | – | – | – | – |
| 04 | **Live** | A game is running. Shows the score, with names when the server sends a roster (05 = 1v1, 06 = no roster). | ↶ Undo | – | – | New game |
| 07 | **Pressed** | Feedback for any key: its label fills in its own colour at once (< 50 ms) and stays filled until the server answers. | | | | |
| 08 | **Goal: who scored?** | Right after a goal, if either team has ≥ 2 players. Same as Live, but the strip turns the scoring team's colour and says "GOAL! Who scored?", and the countdown bar runs. The keys carry the names, outlined in team colours. Each side's players sit above that side's keys. Picking a player of the other team records an own goal. A one-player side (2v1) uses its outer key (K1 or K4) and leaves the inner one empty. | left P1 | left P2 | right P1 | right P2 |
| 09 | **Goal: picked** | After a name is picked, "✓ Name" turns green for 3 s. Pressing another name in those 3 s changes the pick. Then back to Live. | | | | |
| 10 | **Undo?** | After K1 on Live. Shows "8 : 5 → 7 : 5". | Cancel | – | – | ↶ Undo |
| 11 | **End this game?** | After K4 on Live while a game is running. Shows the current score. Save is green, Discard red. | Save | Discard | – | Cancel |
| 12 | **Finished** | The server says the game is over. The winner's half gets a dark team-colour background with "WINNER" in the strip above it. K4 starts a new game straight away (nothing is lost, so it doesn't ask). | ↶ Undo | – | – | New game |
| 13 | **Not sent** | A press got no server confirmation within 5 s. The pressed label shakes, and the strip turns red with "✗ Not sent. Try again." for 3 s. Then the previous screen returns. | (as before) | | | |
| 14 | **No connection** | The server can't be reached for > 10 s while awake. Hollow digits (= "maybe out of date"), amber "No connection" and a crossed Wi-Fi icon, no labels. With no cached score (cold boot), only the strip. | – | – | – | – |
| 15 | **Low battery** | Pack below the warn threshold. Amber battery icon in the strip; nothing else changes. | | | | |
| 16 | **Change batteries** | Pack below the cutoff. Shown for 10 s at boost, then the board sleeps. After that, only a key press (not motion) wakes it, to show this again for 10 s. | – | – | – | – |
| 17 | **Not set up** | The server returns 404 for this device's config. Amber "?", with the device ID for the admin. Retries on every wake. | – | – | – | – |

With 1v1 (each team has one player) the "who scored?" screen is skipped: the scorer is obvious.
In 1v1, an own goal is fixed in the app. Team size always comes from the roster the server sends.
Nothing is hard-coded.

## Motion

Each animation is short (≤ 300 ms, ~10 frames), redraws only a small region (one score half, the
strip or one label) and never delays input. `out/anim.png` shows the frames.

| Event | Animation | Region |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Goal | The scoring side's digit **rolls up**: the old one leaves upward, the new one comes in from below, easing out. At the same time the strip fills with the team colour and the backlight boosts. | one half, 152 × 118 px; strip |
| Undo (confirmed) | The digit **rolls down** (the reverse), then the strip shows "↶ Undone" for 2 s. | one half; strip |
| Who-scored countdown | The bar shrinks, redrawn every 0.5 s. | 320 × 3 px |
| Key press | The label fills at once. No animation: instant is the feedback. | one label |
| Not sent | The pressed label shakes ±4 px, 3 times in 300 ms ("no"), then the red strip appears. | one label; strip |
| Connecting | The lit dot steps along the three dots every 300 ms. | ~40 × 8 px |
| Wake / sleep | The backlight fades in over 400 ms, but only after the first frame is fully drawn (never show uninitialised memory). Going to sleep, it fades out over 400 ms. | backlight only |

No animation loops while nothing is happening. A static screen costs nothing extra on an LCD, but
redraws cost CPU wake-ups.

## Transitions and timeouts

```
          key / motion                       server state
 Black ─────────────► Waking(01/02) ──────────────────► Ready(03) / Live(04) / Finished(12)
   ▲                     │ >10 s no server                       │
   │ table idle          ▼                                        │ goal, a team has ≥ 2 players
   │ (idle_timeout_s) No connection(14) ── server back ──►        ▼
   └──────────── any screen                   Goal(08) ── name ──► Picked(09) ── 3 s ──► Live
                                                 └──── 15 s, no press (scorer stays unset) ──► Live
 Live ─K1─► Undo?(10) ── K4 ──► Live (score −1)        Live ─K4─► End game?(11) ── K1 Save / K2 Discard ──► Ready
               └─ K1 (Cancel) / 8 s ──► Live                        └─ K4 (Cancel) / 8 s ──► Live
```

| Timeout | Value | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Who-scored window | 15 s from the goal | Counted on the server (`last_goal` age), so both boards agree. Afterwards the scorer stays unset and can be fixed in the app. |
| Picked → Live | 3 s | Correction window. |
| Confirm screens (10, 11) | 8 s → Cancel | |
| Press not confirmed | 5 s → Not sent (13) | |
| Not-sent strip | 3 s | |
| "↶ Undone" strip | 2 s | |
| No connection | after 10 s without the server | Retries with backoff (firmware); any key retries at once. |
| Sleep | `idle_timeout_s` (server config) | Backlight fades out, panel off, black. |

## Wrong and odd presses

| Situation | Behaviour | Why |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Key with no label | Nothing happens. No fill, no request. | Rule 2: no box means nothing. |
| Any key while black, Waking, No connection or Not set up | Wakes the board or retries now. It never runs an action. | Nothing is labelled, and the board doesn't know the state yet. |
| Same key twice in a row (bounce, nervous double-tap) | The 2nd press is ignored if it comes < 0.5 s after the screen changed. On 10 and 11, the key you just pressed is **Cancel**. | A double press can never confirm. |
| An unlabelled key on 10/11 | Nothing (rule 2). Only Cancel or the 8 s timeout leaves without acting. | One rule everywhere; the timeout is the safe default. |
| Two keys at once | Ignored, except K1 + any other key, which reads as K1 (K1 shorts the ladder). Firmware accepts a key only if the reading stays in one key's window for the whole press. | A wide finger must not trigger a third key's action. **Constraint:** K1 is never a one-press destructive action. Today it is Undo (it only opens a confirm), Cancel, Save, or a scorer name (correctable for 3 s), so K1 winning is always the safe outcome. |
| A press while a request is still waiting for its answer | Ignored. | One action at a time, no queue. |
| Both boards pressed at once | The server takes the first press. The other board shows the server's new state. The second action is rejected (409); the board shows nothing, it just redraws. | |
| A goal arrives while 10/11 is open | The confirm screen stays open. The score under it updates. Undo removes whatever goal is last when you confirm (the server decides). | |
| A new goal while the who-scored screen is open | Who-scored restarts for the new goal. The previous goal's scorer stays unset. | |

## Battery swap and boot (power can vanish at any instant)

- The board stores **no** UI state that matters. On boot it shows whatever the server says.
- **Cold boot (cells inserted):** draw Waking (02), black with dots, then fade the backlight in.
  Then go straight to the server's screen. With no answer in 10 s, show No connection (14) with only
  the strip. No splash screen, no white flash: fill the panel black before the backlight turns on.
- **Wake from deep sleep:** draw Waking (01) from the score cached in RTC memory, hollow, then fade
  in. RTC memory is a cache only: if it's empty or invalid, use 02.
- Presses during boot or waking are ignored (no labels are shown).
- **Unconfirmed press:** the label stays filled until the server answers. With no answer in 5 s →
  Not sent (13). The press is **dropped, never replayed** (no queue survives a reboot or a
  reconnect). Players press again if they still want it, because replaying an "Undo" later would be
  wrong.

## Drawing rules

- Redraw only what changed: the strip, one score half, the key row. Never redraw the whole screen
  for one change.
- Hollow digits (01, 14) use a 3 px outline in white, so "stale" is visible without colour or
  words.

## What the server must send (input for `firmware/openapi.yaml`)

Already ordered for **this** board (the server knows the board's `end`):
`left`/`right` = `{score, players: [names], color}`; `game`: `none | live | finished`; `winner`:
`left | right | null`; `last_goal`: `{id, side, scorer | null, age_s}`; `can_undo`; and a version
for long-poll. `color` is optional (the defaults above apply). Actions: `undo`,
`new_game {save: true|false}` (when finished: just start one), `assign_scorer {goal_id, player}`.
Who-scored is shown when `last_goal.scorer == null`, `age_s < 15`, and either team has ≥ 2 players
(as screen 08; an own goal in 2v1 is possible).

## Decisions and why (one line each)

| Decision | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Black background, white digits | On an LCD at low backlight, light-on-black reads best and glows least. Big white areas look grey and washed out. |
| Team colour as a bar + names, not the digits | Coloured digits lose half their brightness (red and blue are dark). White is the brightest the panel can show. |
| Team colours from the rods, no team names | "My colour, my side" needs no reading. |
| Left half = the team on the viewer's left at this board | The boards face along the table, so this is what each viewer sees. |
| Four colours with one meaning each (green/red/amber + teams) | Few colours stay learnable. Grey never carries meaning. |
| Every colour cue doubled by shape or words | Colour-blind players and a dim backlight both lose the colour. |
| Idle backlight ≥ 20 % | Below that, the names and labels vanish in a lit room (`out/backlight.png`). |
| Boost 5 s on events, with fades | Draws attention to a change without blinking. Short, for power. |
| Digit roll on goal/undo | Shows *which* side changed and in which direction, in 300 ms. Cheap: one half of the screen. |
| Countdown bar on who-scored | Shows "you have a moment, no rush" without words. |
| Shake on "Not sent" | A universal "no" that points at the key that failed. |
| Only 2 keys in use on Live | Fewer choices = no hesitation. The middle keys stay empty, so no "what does this do?" press. |
| Undo far left, New game far right | A slip lands on an empty key, never on the other action. |
| Who-scored reuses the Live screen | The score stays big after a goal. The names sit over their own team's half, so no new screen to learn. |
| Own goal = pick an opponent's name | No "Own goal" key or sub-screen. The four names are the four people at the table. |
| Ignoring who-scored is fine (15 s) | Play must never wait for the board. The scorer is optional data. |
| Who-scored skipped in 1v1 | The answer is obvious. Don't ask. |
| Confirm puts Cancel on the key you just pressed | A double press or bounce cancels instead of confirming. |
| New game asks Save / Discard / Cancel (user) | Save and Discard are side by side (K1, K2), away from Cancel (K4). |
| No confirm for New game when finished | Nothing is lost, so don't add a step. |
| Hollow digits when not confirmed (waking, offline) | "Maybe old" with no words. The score stays readable. |
| Labels vanish when offline/waking | A key without a label does nothing (rule 2), so no press is ever lost silently. |
| Unconfirmed press is dropped, never replayed | A late Undo or New game would hit the wrong goal or game. |
| Waking shows the cached score, hollow, not a splash | Useful at once, honest that it's not confirmed, and no flicker. |
| Black between matches | Power: an LCD can't show anything for free. |
| Change batteries: 10 s, then only a key press wakes it | Motion wake-ups would drain the dead pack further. |
| Not set up shows the device ID only | The admin needs exactly that, and there's no URL or secret on screen (public repo). |
| Words in English, ≤ 3 per label | The app is in English. Icons (↶, ✓, ✗, Wi-Fi, battery) are added only where nobody would misread them. |

## Hallway test (15 min, 3 colleagues who haven't seen this)

Paper doesn't show a backlit screen. Use a phone at **minimum brightness** in a normally lit room,
showing `out/*.png` at real size (57.6 mm wide) or as close as the phone allows. Tape 4 coins below
it at 15 mm pitch, prop it at 50°, and stand people 1 m away and ~45° to the side. Ask, without
explaining anything first:

1. 04: "What's the score? Which one is your team?" (tell them their colour)
2. 04: "That last goal shouldn't count. Fix it." (expect K1, then K4 on 10)
3. 08: "Carol scored." / "Bob knocked it into his own goal."
4. 04: "You want to quit this game and not count it." (K4, then K2)
5. 01 and 14: "Is this score up to date? What would you do?"
6. 16 and 17: "What does this mean? Who do you call?"
7. 07 and 13: "You pressed Undo and this appeared. What happened?"
8. `anim.png`, one frame after another: "Which side just scored?"

**Pass:** every person answers every task correctly without help, each within ~5 s. Nobody presses
an empty key or a destructive key by mistake. Any task failed by 2 of 3 = redesign that screen.
Write down the exact words people use, and use them in the labels. Repeat tasks 1 and 5 on the real
panel at the chosen idle backlight once there's a prototype.

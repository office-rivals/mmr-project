# Measurements to take at the table

Everything the sled (goal counter) and the scoreboard still need. Measure **one** goal drawer; the other is a
mirror image. "Left/right" = as seen standing at the opening, looking into the tunnel. Distances are measured
**straight in** from the outer face of the black frame. Rough numbers are fine; a photo with a tape measure is
even better.

## Tunnel size
1. **5 cm in:** inside width and inside height (floor to roof).
2. **20 cm in** (just before the goal opening): width and height.
3. Does the black frame have a **step** down to the wooden tunnel? How many mm on each side?
4. Is the floor at the frame **level** with the frame's lower edge, or is there a step up/down (mm)?

## Where the ball drops in
5. How far in are the **near** and **far** edges of the hole the ball drops through?
6. Is that hole the full tunnel width, or off to one side (and how wide)?
7. The slanted plate in photo 3: is it a ramp the ball lands on? Where does it start and end?
8. Optional: a photo straight down through the goal mouth from the playfield.

## Slopes and surface
9. **Along:** put a ball 30 cm in and let go. Does it roll out every time? Slow or fast? (Or a phone level: degrees.)
10. **Across:** phone level laid across the floor (degrees), or the left/right height difference over ~12 cm (mm).
11. Floor surface: bare wood or felt?

## Waiting balls
12. Where does the first waiting ball stop (a lip or a step)? How far in is the front of that ball?
13. **Rebound test:** with 3 balls waiting, drop a 4th through the goal a few times. How far back up the
    tunnel does it bounce before it settles (mm, or "not at all")?

## Scoreboard
14. **End shelf depth** where the scoreboard will stand (front edge to the wall/edge behind). The scoreboard is
    now ~14.8 cm deep.

# Hardware review 2, round 2: the goal counter after the fixes (ee2-fable, 2026-10-18)

Re-checked under the same brief against the current `electronics/*`, `ASSEMBLY.md`, `mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md` + renders,
`firmware/include/board.h`, `firmware/README.md`, INTERFACES §0/§2–4, plus the four files lead allowed (CONSOLIDATED and the
three fix reports). Lead's two late calls are included: the drawer's **keyed wired lead with a slack loop** (no pogo pins) and
beam mode **`a` as the default**. I ran electronics' new checks myself (`check_plugs.py` passes; `perfboard.py`'s body-clash
and wire-routing asserts pass with matplotlib stubbed; `check_wiring.py` needs pyyaml, which isn't installed here, so I
cross-checked every U1/U2 endpoint and header pin order by hand: all match). Datasheets read this round: Kingbright
WP710A10ZGDK and WP710A10SURDK (rev 03/2025), TI LM66100, Waveshare 14657 product page and wiki.

## Verdict

1. **OK to buy.** The cart (`bom.md` *First table (review 2)*) is complete for two counters with spares, every part is an exact
   MPN, and the ones that mattered check out: the green LED **is** InGaN (525 nm, Vf 3.3 V typ, "Green Diffused", current
   datasheet, not obsolete; a distributor page saying "AlGaInP, obsolete" is wrong), the red is AlGaInP 630 nm, the LM66100 is
   1.5–5.5 V, 150 nA, blocks reverse current and has an active-low CE.
2. Every round-1 finding is closed or closed-with-a-bench-gate (table below); nothing is open on the circuit.
3. **Four should-fixes before the first board is soldered or the leads are built**, none of which changes the cart: the J_BAT
   "key" doesn't stop a reversed plug (docs say it does); R_LED_B is 330 Ω in the two drawings and 270 Ω everywhere else;
   ASSEMBLY 3.5 still solders loose headers onto a XIAO that now comes pre-soldered; the Waveshare OLED has a PH2.0 socket, not
   pins, so the OLED-lead step can't be done as written.
4. Confidence unit 1 powers up, flashes and counts on the bench first time: **≈ 92 %** (the SparkFun LIS3DH and named parts
   removed the clone risk; what's left is ordinary soldering).
5. Confidence it runs for months in the table as drawn: **≈ 85 %**, with the rail gate, daylight gate and RSSI test as the
   decisive bench steps and the LM66100 / FPC-antenna fallbacks already in the box.

## New findings (introduced or exposed by the fixes)

### Should-fix before building (none changes the order)

**N1. The J_BAT "key" does not prevent a reversed plug, and the guide says it does.** A 3-cavity housing with the *middle*
cavity plugged is symmetric: turned 180° the plug still sits with the plugged cavity over the pulled middle pin. That is
exactly what `check_plugs.py` L73–76 models ("or reversed there, D1 blocks it"), so electronics knows. But ASSEMBLY L404
("It then fits only J_BAT"), L413 ("the key makes it unlikely"), L475 ("it fits one way") and SLED-ASSEMBLY L168 ("J_BAT's plug
is keyed") tell the builder it can't go on backwards. Reversed it is harmless (D1 blocks, ≈ 2 µA into D0's clamp through the
1 M), but the counter is dead and the troubleshooting row L566 no longer names a reversed plug. The middle-pulled key was chosen
because an *end*-pulled key would let the pack land on a 2-pin header (and GPIO4 at J_BTN has no series resistor): keep that
property. *Fix, pick one:* (a) a key that blocks both: use the 5-cavity housing already in the cart with **cavities 2 and 5
plugged** on a 5-pin J_BAT with **pins 2 and 5 pulled** (+ on 1, − on 4, pin 3 left as a dummy); reversed, cavity 5 lands on
pin 1 and cavity 2 on pin 4, so it can't seat, and on any 2- or 3-pin header at most one cavity touches a pin. Costs 2 header
pins and the spare 5-cavity housings (buy a second 5-pack, ≈ 18 kr) and a re-run of `check_plugs.py` with the new row. Or (b)
keep the layout and fix the words: "fits both ways round: red cavity onto the **+** pin", mark the + side of the housing with
the marker, and add "battery plug reversed → nothing powers, turn it" to troubleshooting L566.

**N2. R_LED_B is 330 Ω in the two drawings the builder works from, 270 Ω everywhere else.** `perfboard.py` L326 ("R_LED_B
330 Ω (D10, boost)", printed under the top view in `perfboard.png`) and `schematic.py` L49 ("330 Ω +5 mA") against README L92,
bom L55, ASSEMBLY L80 and L310, `board.h` L26 and INTERFACES L74 (270 Ω). 330 Ω also appears in the cart only as a *green-LED
tuning* value (bom L54), so a builder reading the picture could fit the wrong part. `check_plugs.py` L15 uses 330 (the softer
path, conservative: fine). *Fix:* change the two labels to 270 and regenerate; or decide 330 and change the five documents.
At 270 + 47 Ω the boost path is (3.3 − 1.3)/317 ≈ 6.3 mA, with R_LED in parallel ≈ 7 mA total: inside GPIO drive and the LED's
rating either way.

**N3. ASSEMBLY 3.5 still builds a XIAO with loose headers; the cart buys the pre-soldered one.** bom L23 (SKU 102010636,
pre-soldered) and ASSEMBLY L26 ("pre-soldered on the XIAO: 14 into the perfboard") versus 3.5 steps 1–4 (L254–261): "push the
two 7-pin headers into the perfboard... lay the XIAO on the headers... solder the 14 pins on the XIAO, then the 14 on the
underside". With pre-soldered headers the sequence is: pad wires first (3.4, unchanged), thread them through (6,6)/(6,7)/(5,8)/
(3,7)/(4,5)/(3,6), drop the XIAO's pins into columns 1–7 rows 3 and 9, solder 14 joints on the underside only, trim. The joint
count table at L26–30 already says so; the step doesn't. *Fix:* rewrite 3.5 (four lines).

**N4. The Waveshare 14657 has a PH2.0 socket, not pins: the OLED lead can't be built as written.** Waveshare's page lists the
module's connector as "PH2.0 connector" and the box contents as "0.91inch OLED Module ×1, PH2.0 4Pin ×1" (a 2.0 mm JST-PH
plug lead; its far-end termination isn't stated). ASSEMBLY L361 and L429–430, `wiring.yml` L130 and SLED-ASSEMBLY L81 say
"four jumpers... the other ends onto the module's pins by its silk". *Fix:* use the supplied PH2.0 lead at the module end: if
its far end is Dupont-style crimps, click them straight into the keyed 5-cavity housing (SCL G SDA V, cavity 5 plugged); if it
is bare or too short (the route is ≈ 200 mm), splice it to four jumper crimps with heat-shrink. Keep the "meter the lead before
the first plug-in" step; add "measure the cable on arrival" to SLED-ASSEMBLY §9. The AliExpress fleet module (lead's cut) will
most likely have a 2.54 mm header, so the guide should cover both. Also unverified on the Waveshare page: regulator, power
LED, sleep current; the bench's "OLED unplugged vs plugged" reading (ASSEMBLY L533–535) settles it.

### Nice

- **N5. LM66100 fitting instructions.** It is bought ahead (bom L33, ASSEMBLY L93–94) but no step says how to fit it if the gate
  fails: on the PA0087 adapter, IN → (20,2)'s VPACK, OUT → (20,3)'s VSYS, GND → GND, **CE → GND** (CE higher than VIN disables
  it; TI), ST open, flying leads, hot-glued flat; D1 removed. Five lines in README or ASSEMBLY §7 save a re-review on the bench.
- **N6. Antenna keep-out, made permanent.** Move R_BTN back to (10,4)–(10,5) (free holes, x 31.2, 7 mm from the XIAO) and add a
  `perfboard.py` assert that no part body lies within 5 mm of the XIAO's far edge (dimensions.md L18 is the rule; nothing
  enforces it). R_S8/R_S2 are at the corners and can stay if the RSSI test is fine.
- **N7. Green LED headroom.** Vf 3.3 V typ at 20 mA means ≈ 2.6–2.9 V at 1–2 mA; GPIO5 HIGH gives ≈ 3.1 V after R_S, so the green
  runs at ≈ 1 mA through 220 Ω and a high-Vf unit may be dim. The 150 Ω tuning value is in the cart (bom L54); note in §6.6 that
  R_G may go down to 0 Ω safely (R_S 100 Ω still limits the pin).
- **N8. Stale rows left after the fixes** (owners): README L27–38 block diagram (22 k, 68 k/100 k, no R_S), L274 ("provisional
  3.45 V cutoff"), L291–293 ("a_boost/b add 3.1–3.25 mA", now 5.3); INTERFACES L71 (clone first), L80 ("resistors socketed"),
  L81 ("J_OLED G V SCL SDA"), L96 ("alkaline/lithium OK"), L97 ("≤ 30 µA acceptance"), L104 ("battery lead via JST-PH");
  ASSEMBLY L112 ("three small headers": four).
- **N9. Cart odds and ends.** One SparkFun SEN-13963 spare (63 kr; it is the only module with none, and the reference build
  waits 2–3 days if one is DOA); the 2 small zip ties SLED-ASSEMBLY L73 asks for. Hot glue does not bond to silicone insulation,
  so the tab-groove "strain relief" (SLED-ASSEMBLY L137–139) works only if the glue fills the groove around the lead as a
  mechanical plug; a small zip tie or a knot at the tab is surer.
- **N10. STAT plug reversed on J_STAT** (3-pin, no key): both LEDs reverse-biased at 3.3 V, nothing lights, nothing harmed
  (VR 5 V). Add "no status blink at all → turn the STAT plug" to troubleshooting.

**Remaining original blockers — B3 and B4**

The 3.1 V power gate is appropriate, but the presently selected threshold fails its own conservative arithmetic. With the stated 0.70 V path loss, **3.80 V resting pack** would be required even before residual measurement error is budgeted. That is an illustrative bound, not a recommended final cutoff. Measure the actual complete path, including low/cold cells, bridged holder switch, loop, contacts and MCU regulator. Establish the threshold from the minimum loaded rail with allowance for calibration/nonlinearity/temperature, and recalculate usable capacity. A 2.76 V brownout reset does not enforce the ESP32-C6's 3.0 V operating minimum. [ESP32-C6 electrical characteristics](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-c6_datasheet_en.pdf).

Do not assume sound and radio are mutually exclusive: `electronics/README.md:253` says “never together,” but the authorized firmware fix report explicitly says an arriving chirp is not delayed when a POST is already on air (`firmware/review2-fixes-2026-10-18.md:21–23`). Test that sequence, reconnects and background radio traffic with the display active.

For optics, retaining default `a` is compatible with qualifying and, where necessary, screening each first-table unit. It is not compatible with treating the default resistor as a guaranteed minimum-part link. The present model is extrapolated from emitter intensity at 100 mA and receiver response at VCE=5 V; neither guarantees transfer at this low-current/low-voltage operating point. Qualify the chosen resistor and per-table mode across clear, ball-blocked and transition conditions at the lowest rail and worst accepted daylight angle. Record an explicit lighting/alignment envelope and actual scope traces; a mode change cannot create contrast when both readings are saturated. [TSAL6200 characteristics](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81010/tsal6200.pdf), [TEFT4300 characteristics and switching test conditions](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81549/teft4300.pdf).

**NEW blocker R2-B1 — LM66100 is not a guaranteed AA-charger isolator in this topology, and its integration is missing.**

`electronics/bom.md:33` buys LM66100DCKR/PA0087 as the D1 replacement; `electronics/README.md:215–217` says it prevents the onboard charger charging AAs. Consider battery operation first, so its channel is already ON, followed by USB insertion. The XIAO charger is configured for about 120 mA. [Seeed XIAO power schematic](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/SeeedStudio-XIAO-ESP32C6/XIAO_ESP32_C6_v1.0_SCH_260114.pdf).

With CE connected to VOUT, TI specifies a turn-off differential of **35 mV typical, 80 mV maximum**, R_ON **91 mΩ typical at 3.6 V**, and reverse activation current **0.5 A typical, 1 A maximum**. Thus 120 mA reverse flow produces only about **11 mV** across the conducting switch. It need not trip: a depleted AA pack can accept the charger's current while the channel remains on. This is a datasheet-based operating counterexample, not a measured failure; it defeats the claimed guarantee. The low OFF-state leakage is irrelevant until the channel actually turns off. [TI LM66100, §§6.5 and 8.3.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm66100.pdf).

Separately, this is a six-pin IC, not a two-terminal diode substitution. There is no completed connection/mounting instruction in the permitted schematic, perfboard, wiring or assembly files. PA0087 is a package adapter, not a finished power-path module. [Chip Quik PA0087 drawing](https://www.chipquik.com/datasheets/PA0087.pdf).

**Fix:** retain D1 until a replacement has a complete schematic, layout, insulation and assembly procedure, plus guaranteed behaviour with this current-limited charger. Use a suitable power-path controller or explicit USB-dependent disconnection that prevents reverse flow before it starts. Validate battery-first/USB-first attach and detach with fresh and depleted pack equivalents; measure steady and transient reverse current. Remove the present “drop-in/charger cannot charge AAs” approval from the order/build instructions. Resolve dropout and charger isolation together.

**NEW blocker R2-B2 — Plan C loses the fastest ball and cannot recover saturated optical readings.**

`firmware/include/board.h:63–66` sets 10 ms chopped reads and 400 µs settling per LED step. `firmware/README.md:98–102` says D8 is ignored while the application polls the off/on difference. The design's smallest/fastest-ball example gives **(31−3) mm / 3 m/s = 9.33 ms** useful interruption. Blocked-state samples 10 ms apart cannot guarantee observing that event at an arbitrary phase; they also cannot establish its duration with the existing 5 ms qualification margin. A successful set of slowly rolled balls would not test this failure mode.

There is a separate analogue limit. With both pull-ups active, `220k || 68k = 51.94k`. Near VCE≈0.3 V, the node can sink only approximately **(3.3−0.3)/51.94k = 58 µA** from those pull-ups. Ambient strong enough to pin the LED-OFF node near saturation leaves little or no on/off voltage difference. Subtraction cannot recover the missing signal. This is a load-line illustration, not a guaranteed saturation boundary; measure the actual receiver. The source of that limitation is still the DC receiver circuit.

**Fix:** qualify a substantially faster complete off/on acquisition and detector timing against the shortest ball at every sampling phase; 1–2 ms is a reasonable starting interval to evaluate, not an approval. Define required ADC headroom and a minimum clear-beam difference at the lowest rail. If the actual table exceeds that envelope, change shielding/gain or use a qualified modulated light-barrier receiver. Plan C may help within a bounded nonsaturated range, but it cannot close B4 as currently documented. Also measure the handover after two health checks; goals must remain detectable while the system waits to enter the fallback.

**NEW should-fix R2-S1 — The new lead is neither positively polarized nor specified for its repeated bend/tether duty.**

`ASSEMBLY.md:402–405,475–476` claims the three-position middle key fits one way. The pattern **[+ / blocked / −]** is symmetric: rotating the plug swaps +/− while the middle still matches. It distinguishes the header, not polarity. Use an asymmetric key or a genuinely polarized connector, and verify its envelope/retention. Preserve the wired slack-loop decision.

`electronics/bom.md:40` specifies only generic 24 AWG two-core silicone. `mechanical/sled.py:59,217–228` assumes Ø2.8 mm and a **5.6 mm bend radius**, while `mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md:160–162` makes the electrical lead the drawer's travel tether. The model has only **2 mm straight lead between the moving tab and U tangent at 125 mm travel** (`a−b = 64.5−d/2`); that reaches zero at 129 mm. This does not prove breakage, but leaves little overpull allowance and relies on a free cable following an ideal trajectory. Ten hand cycles (`ASSEMBLY.md:411`) do not establish repeated service life.

**Fix:** select the actual flexible wire, document its outside envelope and bend capability, and use a mechanical travel stop or separate tether before the conductors/terminations take tension. Retain proper slack and mechanically secure both strain-relief points. Cycle and tug the assembled harness, checking abrasion, crimp movement and voltage drop. If insulation damage can expose an unfused battery short, add appropriate near-holder protection and include its resistance in B3; D1 does not protect an upstream short.

**NEW should-fix R2-S2 — Drawer withdrawal no longer removes battery power, invalidating service and test steps.**

`ASSEMBLY.md:554–555` still says withdrawal powers off and reinsertion boots. `ASSEMBLY.md:457` and `firmware/README.md:235` use “drawer out” as a USB-only fixture. The new loop remains connected throughout withdrawal; neither statement is true. A leakage test performed this way measures a real attached pack and can conceal a failed isolation assumption.

**Fix:** say explicitly **remove cells or disconnect J_BAT** for battery-absent and restart tests, and disconnect the bench supply before substituting cells. Explain that merely opening the drawer leaves the counter live. Test USB-only buzzer behaviour with the battery actually absent, and USB-plus-battery isolation separately. `mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md:167` already correctly attributes power loss to removing cells.

**NEW should-fix R2-S3 — Fixes have left competing component values and connector maps in build documents.**

The revised cart specifies **R_LED_B=270 Ω** (`electronics/bom.md:55`; also `ASSEMBLY.md:310` and `board.h`), but the current schematic source/SVG and perfboard source/render still say **330 Ω** (`electronics/schematic.py:49`, `electronics/perfboard.py:326`). A builder following either drawing will fit a different emitter current from the optical-margin calculation.

The newly rearranged OLED header is **SCL/GND/SDA/VCC/key** (`electronics/perfboard.py:58,66`, `ASSEMBLY.md:429–430`), whereas `team/INTERFACES.md:81` still says **G/V/SCL/SDA**. Other remaining conflicts include lithium allowed at `INTERFACES.md:96`, ≤30 µA sleep acceptance at `:97`, JST-PH at `:104`, and the old boost-energy paragraph at `electronics/README.md:290–293`. Its updated table gives **27 days** pessimistic boosted life at `:299`, yet `:302` says boost clears the one-month target.

**Fix:** reconcile the current sources and regenerate drawings, then check the actual values and connector-to-net mapping in each output. Make the first-table parts and tested modes explicit; remove obsolete assembly coordinates and runtime claims instead of leaving several plausible instructions. Count the measured Plan C load if that mode is needed.

**NEW should-fix R2-S4 — Enclosure clearance evidence does not represent the revised board population.**

The electrical board layout moved multiple headers and components, but `mechanical/sled.py:350–351` still approximates tall parts by x=52…64/y=22…28 and plugs by x=20…52/y=2…4.8 in board coordinates. Current J_OLED/J_STAT are at y≈26.26, J_BZ at y≈11.02, and C1 is near x=59.14/y=17.37 (`electronics/perfboard.py:45,54,58,60`). Those actual heights/locations are not covered by the stated boxes; `mechanical/sled.py:368` uses those boxes in `in_l`. This is incomplete clearance evidence, not an asserted new collision.

**Fix:** update component/plug/lead envelopes from the selected board build, including worst-case capacitor dimensions, socket/header stack, wire bends and any eventual power adapter. Verify lid, wiring and antenna clearances with those envelopes, then dry-fit the actual populated board before duplicating the print. Keep the now-correct Q2/LIS3DH separation.

**First-unit bench checklist — complete before building the second**

1. **Incoming and dry-fit:** confirm module revisions, perfboard grid, polarity/pinouts, capacitor envelope, keyed plug orientation and the selected loop wire. Fit all connectors and leads under the lid; exercise full drawer travel and overpull restraint.
2. **Limited power and calibration:** unpowered continuity/polarity first; then limited 4 V power, rail and ADC checks. Verify calibration near the eventual cutoff, not only at fresh-pack voltage. Record actual parts and resistor values.
3. **Power envelope:** scope MCU 3V3 through the entire real holder/lead path during worst TX/reconnect, display and sound overlap, with source resistance and temperature allowance. Require ≥3.1 V at the accepted cutoff and update the usable-capacity estimate.
4. **USB and reversal:** distinguish battery absent from drawer open. Test both connection orders, USB-only sound, no-pack ADC, measured reverse battery current and current-limited reversed input. Any replacement power path must pass before fitting AA cells behind it.
5. **Optics:** measure clear/blocked voltages and edge/recovery timing at low rail, all accepted modes, worst daylight angles and realistic dust/misalignment. Test fastest balls across sampling phases, including health-read windows and Plan C entry/exit. Record ADC headroom and contrast, not only counts.
6. **Bus, sound and energy:** verify far-end I²C rise time/levels, Q2 saturation and coil current, indicators' true off state, C1 leakage, baseline sleep and integrated full-device sleep. Measure charge during normal play and network failure before claiming runtime.
7. **Installed endurance:** both goals, closed panels and players present; record RF margin/reconnects. Repeat drawer/connector cycling and table impacts during radio activity; inspect insulation, anchors and joints, then repeat rail/optical tests.

# Independent hardware review 2: the goal counter, before buying

Reviewer: ee2-fable, 2026-10-18. Read per the brief (`team/briefs/ee-review-2.md`): the electronics folder, ASSEMBLY, SLED-ASSEMBLY, sled renders, `firmware/include/board.h`, `firmware/README.md`, INTERFACES §0 and §2–4. No design history. Checked online: Vishay TEFT4300 (doc 81549), Same Sky CMT-1209-390T, Mill-Max 0906, SG Micro SGM6029, and Seeed's `XIAO-ESP32-C6_v1.0_SCH` (part names extracted from the PDF).

## Verdict

1. **Buy after the order-list fixes B1–B3** (undercounted housings, headers and socket strips; unnamed status-LED and pogo-pin parts; a keyed OLED plug). Nothing in the circuit itself blocks the order.
2. Every net, pin and voltage checks out. Seeed's schematic confirms the power path the README relies on: SGM6029 buck (1.95–5.5 V in, Iq 2.3 µA), SGM40567 charger (ICharge = 24000/200 k = 120 mA), LN237 PMOS battery switch, VBUS through an LMBR4010 Schottky. The 1N5817 does what it is there for.
3. Confidence that unit 1 powers up, flashes and counts balls on the bench first time: **≈ 90 %**. The residual is the unverified AliExpress LIS3DH clone and min-spec optics (the sockets cover that).
4. Confidence it then runs for months in the table as drawn: **≈ 75 %** until the bench closes S1 (chirp + Wi-Fi at the 3.45 V cutoff), S2 (buzzer parts inside the accelerometer footprint), S4 (unsecured plugs) and S5 (a bare pack on the drawer's back).
5. No plug reversal fries anything except J_OLED (B3). B1–B3 are an hour of BOM and doc work; S1–S5 are small and should land before unit 2.

## Facts verified (so the next reader doesn't have to)

- **XIAO pins.** D0–D10 = GPIO 0, 1, 2, 21, 22, 23, 16, 17, 19, 20, 18; back pads MTMS/MTDI/MTCK/MTDO = GPIO 4/5/6/7; user LED GPIO15 (active LOW, 1.5 k), RF-switch power GPIO3 (LOW = on, 10 k pull-up R21), antenna select GPIO14 (5.1 k pull-down = ceramic). Matches README L262–283 and `board.h`.
- **ESP32-C6.** LP GPIOs are 0–7 (ext1 deep-sleep wake), ADC1_CH0–CH6 on GPIO0–6: D0 = CH0, D2 = CH2, and GPIO4/5/6 are LP, as used. Strapping pins are GPIO4/5 (SDIO slave sampling/driving edge only), 8/9 (boot), 15 (JTAG source). GPIO4/5 used for the button and LEDs: harmless, as README L278–280 says. USB-JTAG is on GPIO12/13 (not exposed). GPIO16 = U0TXD (D6, pull-up B through 22 k: ROM boot chatter is harmless), GPIO17 = U0RXD (D7, the 10 k strap). ext1 on the C6 supports a wake level per pin (firmware's job). I could not extract the reset-time pull table from the datasheet PDF; a default pull-up on GPIO4 is fine (100 k external agrees), a default pull-down on GPIO5 only gives the red LED ≈ 35 µA for the boot milliseconds.
- **Power path.** On batteries the pack feeds the BAT pad through D1; on USB the PMOS opens, the charger drives the BAT pad (= VSYS, C1) to 4.2 V and D1 blocks it from the pack. Reversed pack: D1 blocks; the 1 M divider puts ≈ 1.7 µA into D0's clamp. Both fine. VBAT sense reads the pack before D1, so "no pack" on USB works.
- **Optics (TEFT4300 datasheet):** Ica 0.8 min / 3.2 typ mA at 1 mW/cm², VCE 5 V; ICEO 1 typ / 200 max nA; ±30°; VCEO 70 V, VECO 5 V. README L63–75 uses the right numbers; the 73 µA typical / 0.91× min-case conclusion stands. Reversed sensor plug puts 3.3 V on E-C: under the 5 V VECO.
- **Buzzer (CMT-1209-390T datasheet):** 3.0 V rated, 2.0–5.0 V, 2048 Hz, 65 mA at rated square-wave drive, coil 13/16/19 Ω, 85/90 dBA at 10 cm. Passive (externally driven), so the PWM volume scheme works. BC337-40 pinout C-B-E (flat face) and the D3 orientation (band to VSYS) in ASSEMBLY L152–154 are right.
- **Pogo pins (Mill-Max 0906 datasheet):** 2 A continuous / 3 A peak, 20 mΩ, ≈ 60 g at mid-stroke, 1,000,000 cycles. Stroke is 1.4 mm (0.055"), not the "≥ 1.5" in bom L40: with 0.75 mm compression that is still mid-stroke. Fine; relax the spec line.
- **Netlist.** I cross-checked every net in `perfboard.py` NETS (L66–99) against ASSEMBLY 3.6 (L257–282), `wiring.yml` and `schematic.py`, hole by hole (ASSEMBLY is 1-based, perfboard.py 0-based). They agree, including the six pad-wire holes and the J_STAT / J_OLED pin orders.

## Findings

### Blockers (to the order; the circuit is fine)

**B1. The counted cart is short of connector parts.** `bom.md` L96–99 and L38 buy 2-pin housings × 10, 3-pin × 3, one 1 × 40 male header and one 1 × 20 machined socket strip. Per counter the drawings need: 4 × 2-pin (BAT, LED, BTN, BZ), **2 × 3-pin** (sensor plug, `wiring.yml` L104–110, and STAT, L135–140), **1 × 4-pin** (OLED, L129–134: not in the cart at all), 20 male header pins (J_PT 2, J_LED 2, J_BAT 2, J_BTN 2, J_BZ 2, J_OLED 4, J_STAT 3, mouth joint 3) and 10 machined socket pins (1 × 4 + 1 × 2 + 1 × 4, ASSEMBLY L142–143, L158). Two counters: 40 header pins = the whole strip, 20 socket pins = the whole strip, and cutting a machined strip destroys one pin per cut (≈ 6 cuts). Also 4-pin housings × 0 and 3-pin × 3 against 4 needed. *Fix:* 3-pin housings × 6, 4-pin × 3 (or 5-pin × 3, see B3), male header strips × 2, machined socket strips × 2. Fold the "16 per counter" (ASSEMBLY L333) / "19" (bom L39) / "6" (ASSEMBLY L82) jumper counts into one number: 19.

**B2. Two parts are specified by properties, not part numbers.** (a) Status LEDs, bom L207: "3 mm diffused true green, InGaN, not GaP yellow-green". This matters: a GaP 568 nm green (Vf ≈ 1.9 V) in series with the red leaves the pair conducting tens of µA at 3.3 V with the pin floating, i.e. a faint glow and a sleep-current hit; the InGaN pair does not (README L280). Most "3 mm green diffused" DigiKey hits are GaP. (b) Pogo pins, bom L40: "Mill-Max solder-cup, pick at order". *Fix:* name both in the cart before ordering. For the pins, a Mill-Max 0906-series solder-cup spring pin (standard stroke, 1.4 mm; 60 g mid-stroke; 2 A) is the match; for the green LED pick one whose datasheet says InGaN / 520–530 nm / Vf ≥ 2.8 V at 20 mA and that is diffused; pair it with any AlInGaP 620–630 nm diffused red. Then send mechanical the pin's barrel diameter (dimensions.md L63 asks for it).

**B3. J_OLED is the one plug that can do damage when reversed.** It is a plain 4-pin header (perfboard.py L56, ASSEMBLY L156) carrying GND, 3V3, SCL, SDA, and the lead is 4 loose F–F jumpers in a 4-pin housing (`wiring.yml` L129–134). Turned 180° the module's VCC is driven from SCL and its GND lands on SDA; a VCC/GND swap at the module end does the same (the silk note at L46 only helps if read). Every other plug is harmless reversed (BTN through its 1 k, LED and sensor through their resistors, BAT through D1, STAT reverse-biases two LEDs, BZ is a coil). *Fix:* key it the way the sensor plug is keyed: a 5-pin header with pin 5 pulled, a 5-cavity housing with cavity 5 plugged (adds 5-pin housings × 3 to B1), and a bench line "meter the lead before the first plug-in: black must land on the module's GND pin".

### Should-fix (before unit 2 goes into the table)

**S1. Goal chirp and goal POST coincide at the cutoff.** firmware/README L98 chirps "at once" and POSTs at once. At the 3.45 V cutoff: TX ≈ 250 mA (board.h L83, 15 dBm) plus the buzzer ≈ 180 mA peak at a 3.1 V VSYS (16 Ω coil), ≈ 0.43 A through D1 → Vf ≈ 0.35 V, plus ≈ 0.05 V in the cells → VSYS ≈ 3.05 V, and the buck at 100 % duty gives ≈ 2.95–3.0 V on 3V3: at or under the C6's 3.0 V minimum (README L205–208 already says "zero margin on paper"). The brown-out reset (2.5 V) won't fire; a Wi-Fi hiccup or a failed POST can. *Fix, cheapest first:* (1) firmware: don't overlap them, e.g. start the POST when the 220 ms chirp ends (ux's 0.24 s goal→sound budget is unaffected; the server sees the goal 0.2 s later), or play Medium on low battery; (2) raise `LOW_BATT_CUTOFF_MV` to 3600 (1.2 V/cell, ≈ 3–5 % of NiMH capacity); (3) a lower-Vf 3 A Schottky in D1's place only buys ≈ 70 mV. The bench item in `firmware/README` L201–202 and L213–214 decides. A scope-free version is in the bench list below.

**S2. The buzzer driver sits inside the accelerometer footprint.** perfboard.py L53–54 puts R_BD at (19,4)–(19,5) and Q2 at (18,6)–(20,6); the LIS3DH outline (L256–257) spans x 32.5–55.3, y 9.7–22.4, and those holes are at x 51.5–54.1, y 13.6–18.6, i.e. inside it (the top view shows the labels overlapping the red box). With the **SparkFun fallback** on headers at cols 13–18 (ASSEMBLY L206–213) a standing 100 k and a TO-92 collide with its PCB: the fallback no longer fits. With the **clone glued flat** (L205) it depends on the clone's length: ≤ 17 mm fits left of col 18, the common 20+ mm "LIS3DSH LIS3DH" boards don't. *Fix:* move Q2 and R_BD one row up into the free row 7 (cols 18–23 are empty there) and re-run `perfboard.py`, or state in 3.2 that the clone must be ≤ 17 mm long and glued at the left of the outline, and that the SparkFun fallback needs the move. Do this before the first board is soldered; it is a 10-minute layout change now and a desoldering job later.

**S3. The ≤ 30 µA sleep acceptance can't pass with the status LED blinking.** ASSEMBLY L498 requires ≤ 30 µA; README L240 budgets the asleep blink at 2 mA × 30 ms / 5 s ≈ 12 µA average plus the LP core, on top of the 22 µA. A DMM on the µA range will read 30–40 µA and jump every 5 s. *Fix:* measure with the STAT plug pulled (or `blinks` 0, firmware/README L207–208) for the acceptance figure, then plugged for the real figure; write both into ASSEMBLY §7.2.

**S4. Seven Dupont plugs, no retention, in a box that gets hit by balls.** ASSEMBLY L450 says "don't hot-glue or tape anything yet" and §6.7 / §8 never come back to the plugs; SLED-ASSEMBLY L93 just plugs them. Single-row Dupont housings on 0.1" pins creep off under vibration. *Fix:* after calibration (ASSEMBLY 6.7) a dab of hot glue over the shoulder of each plug (it peels for service), or a zip tie around the J_BAT/J_PT/J_LED group. Note it in SLED-ASSEMBLY §4.3 too.

**S5. The pack has no short-circuit protection and bare contacts on the drawer's back.** The two 8 × 8 nickel targets are glued *flush* (dimensions.md L64, ASSEMBLY L384), 10 mm apart, on a drawer that is carried to a table with 3 charged NiMH in it: a key, coin or steel ruler bridges them. 3 × AA NiMH deliver > 10 A into a short; the 26 AWG holder leads and the targets' solder joints get very hot. The sled side is fine (the pins are recessed in the lane wall). *Fix, zero electrical cost (preferred):* mechanical adds a 1–1.5 mm raised rib between the two targets so a flat object can't bridge them. *Alternative:* a 0.5–0.75 A-hold PTC in the holder's red lead inside the drawer, which costs ≈ 0.1–0.2 Ω (≈ 60 mV at a TX peak: it eats into S1's margin).

**S6. Metal within 2–5 mm of the XIAO's antenna.** dimensions.md L18 says keep metal ≥ 5 mm from the antenna end (board x ≈ 20–24). J_STAT (perfboard.py L58) is at x 26.1 with a Dupont housing on it, the R_G/R_R socket at 28.7 and R_BTN at 31.2. *Fix:* on the bench compare RSSI with the STAT plug on and off and with the lid on; if it costs > 3 dB, put R_R/R_G inline in the lead at the LEDs (heat-shrunk, like the button's 1 k) and drop the socket, and move J_STAT right or onto the bottom row. Cheap to test, cheap to change before unit 2.

**S7. ASSEMBLY's "Parts" checklist (L67–88) is from before phase 1.** It lists 6 jumpers, a 3 × 2-pin header, one 1 × 4 socket, no 680 Ω / 22 k / 1 k / 2 × 100 k, and no buzzer, BC337, D3, MX switch, OLED, status LEDs, pogo pins or nickel strip, while 3.1 and §4 use all of them. A hobbyist ticking that list starts the build short. *Fix:* regenerate the checklist from 3.1 + §4 (or point it at the bom's per-device table and delete the duplicate).

**S8. The buzzer lead runs untwisted beside the 220 k sensor node.** W_BZ (`wiring.yml` L123–128) has no `twisted pair`, unlike W_LED and W_PT, and it shares the dash duct with W_PTX (ASSEMBLY L333–336) over ≈ 200 mm. A 4 V, 2 kHz square wave at 180 mA next to a 220 k ‖ 1 nF node couples ≈ tens of mV per edge: not a false goal by itself (V_IL is 0.8 V above a 0.2 V lit level), but it is free to avoid. *Fix:* twist W_BZ (1 turn/cm) and lay it on the far side of the duct from W_PTX; the §6.6 "screen on, no false goals" test should also run with 20 chirps.

**S9. TEFT4300 lead identification rests on one drawing note.** README L79–81, dimensions.md L115 and ASSEMBLY L360 say "collector = the shorter lead, on the flange-flat side". I could not read the drawing from the datasheet text, and the sensor is committed into a keyed 3-pin housing (green = collector) before the beam test. If the note is wrong, every unit fails step 6.5 and needs its crimps swapped. *Fix:* a 30-second meter check in §4.3 before soldering: diode mode, red probe on the suspected collector, black on the emitter, under a desk lamp: the reading drops well below the dark reading; swapped probes don't react. Then crimp.

### Nice

- **N1. C1 470 µF leakage.** README L177 wants ≤ 2 µA after 10 min; the UVR series spec allows 0.01 CV = 47 µA (2 min). Real parts settle to a few µA at 4 V. If a unit misses the 30 µA acceptance, the cap is the first suspect: a 100 µF low-leakage part (or 2 × 47 µF MLCC, zero leakage) covers the µs bounce the cap is there for (README L173–176) and leaks nothing.
- **N2. Lithium L91 cells.** 3 × 1.8 V open circuit = 5.4 V against the SGM6029's 5.5 V maximum (README L155–158): 0.1 V of margin. Say "NiMH or alkaline; L91 only if you accept running at the buck's limit".
- **N3. 100 nF across J_BTN** (as the scoreboard's optional note, ASSEMBLY L612): the button is a 220 mm unshielded lead into a 100 k node with ext1 wake on it; 10 ms of RC keeps RF and ESD ticks out of the "scan" sound.
- **N4. Stale "12 × 12 tactile"** in board.h L36 and SLED-ASSEMBLY L74 (the switch is an MX blue since 2026-10-18).
- **N5. ASSEMBLY 3.2 step 4** solders the clone's wire into a hole and "the underside wire joins it later": a second wire into a filled hole is fiddly. Twist the clone wire with its net wire and solder once, or solder the net wires first.
- **N6. USB-C plug size.** The lane clears a 13 × 8 overmould (dimensions.md L59); say "use a slim cable" in ASSEMBLY §9 and the tools list. Many braided cables are 14+ mm wide.
- **N7. Tools list** lacks the superglue (ASSEMBLY L371), a marker (L163) and a bench supply / scope that the README's own open item (L209–211) assumes; the bench list below gives a scope-free substitute.
- **N8. Beam node vs Wi-Fi.** The sensor pair ends ≈ 40 mm from the antenna through the dash. 2.4 GHz rectified in the phototransistor shifts the lit level during TX; the 1 nF helps. Bench: read D8 during a forced TX burst (the per-goal POST) with the beam lit.

## Challenges (what I would do differently, and what it changes)

- **C1. Delete the role strap.** The strap guards against flashing the scoreboard image onto a goal board (README L285–309), and that image was removed on 2026-10-18 (ASSEMBLY L412–413). Today it is a resistor, a wire, a GPIO and a boot check whose only failure mode is "forgot R_STRAP → WRONG BOARD, device refuses to run". Drop it, move the buzzer from the MTDO pad to D7 (not LP, no wake needed), and the XIAO needs 5 pad wires instead of 6. Re-add it when a second image exists. *Trade-off:* one less guard against a future mistake.
- **C2. Delete the USB-sense divider.** It exists so a sleeping device wakes and stays awake for flashing (README L271). The flasher already has the drawer out and the undo button at the opening: press it, the device is awake for `idle_timeout_s` (300 s), and firmware can tell USB is attached from the USB-Serial-JTAG controller itself (`usb_serial_jtag_is_connected()`) to hold off light sleep. Saves R_VB1/R_VB2, the MTCK pad wire and a GPIO. *Trade-off:* one manual press before `-t upload`; no unattended wake on plug-in.
- **C3. Resistors in the leads, not in sockets, for the status LEDs** (and keep the beam sockets, which are real knobs). The 1 × 4 socket + two standing resistors sit at the antenna end (S6); inline in the heat-shrunk lead, as the button's 1 k already is, they vanish from the board. *Trade-off:* brightness tuning becomes two solder joints instead of a swap; do it once on unit 1 and copy.
- **C4. Beam boost (D10, R_LED_B).** `a_boost` is "the same LED, brighter". The socketed R_LED already does that by hand; the pin only makes it remote. If a GPIO is ever needed, this is the one to give up. Keep it for now: it costs one resistor.
- **C5. D1 1N5817.** Right part for the job (blocks the 120 mA charger, ≈ 0.15 V at the average current). A PMOS ideal diode would save ≈ 0.3 V at TX peaks and ≈ 1 cell-week of life, for two parts and a more delicate layout: not worth it; fix S1 in firmware instead.
- **C6. C1.** See N1: 100 µF low-leakage or MLCC. Same bounce cover, no leakage, half the height.
- **C7. The drawer contacts.** The pogo + nickel scheme is good: reverse-safe, the drawer is the switch, no cable to break. Keep it; just the rib (S5) and the named pin (B2).
- **Decisions I would keep as they are:** DC beam over a 38 kHz carrier (right at 1 mA and this geometry; the health ADC covers daylight); buck-only power from the XIAO (an external buck-boost would cost 4–5× the sleep current, README L165–168); the LIS3DH clone with the ≤ 30 µA acceptance; the low-side BC337 buzzer from VSYS (keeps the 250 mA pulses off the 3V3 rail); R_PU/R_LED in sockets; the 10 k, not a wire, on D7 if the strap stays.

## Bench checklist: unit 1, before unit 2 is built

Order of the day: 1–6 on the bare board, 7–12 in the sled on the bench, 13–16 in the table.

1. **Visual + continuity** (ASSEMBLY 3.8) plus: J_OLED lead metered end to end (black → module GND pin); TEFT4300 collector found with the meter under a lamp (S9) *before* its housing is made.
2. **Pack through D1:** J_BAT+ = pack; VSYS = pack − 0.1–0.3; 3V3 = 3.25–3.35; D0 = pack/2; 5V pin and the divider middle < 0.5 V. Then USB only, drawer out: divider middle ≈ 2.9 V, VSYS ≈ 4.2 V (the charger), charge LED blinking.
3. **First flash with R_STRAP fitted** (D7 is U0RXD; confirm the USB download still works with it held low). Log shows the LIS3DH found at 0x18/0x19 and no `LIS3DSH`.
4. **Sleep current, two readings** (S3): STAT plug off → ≤ 30 µA (acceptance; ≥ 10 min after sleep); STAT plug on → ≈ 30–40 µA average with 5 s pulses. Also: pull the OLED plug and note the difference (its sleep current, EST 5–20 µA).
5. **Wake:** still for 10 min → no wake; a brisk 10° tilt → wake; INT1 reads HIGH until cleared.
6. **Status LEDs:** green every 2 s awake, Hi-Z between blinks shows no glow and no current; a GaP-green glow here means B2 went wrong.
7. **Beam, lit and blocked, room lights on** (ASSEMBLY 6.5): D8 < 0.4 V lit, > 2.9 V with a ball; then a phone torch into the frame opening with the ball in place: still > 2.9 V (or switch to mode `b` and re-check). Note the health numbers in µA per mode.
8. **Beam during a chirp and a TX** (S8, N8): hold the beam lit, trigger 20 chirps and 20 POSTs, log must show no `GOAL`.
9. **50 balls** from a slow roll to a hard throw: 50 `GOAL`, 50 chirps, no doubles.
10. **Low-pack run, no scope needed** (S1): power through J_BAT from a bench supply at 3.45 V, or from 3 NiMH rested at ≈ 1.15 V/cell; sound High; 20 goals with Wi-Fi up. The reset reason in the metrics must stay `power-on` (no `brownout`), and every goal must arrive. Repeat at 3.6 V. Pick the cutoff from this.
11. **Drawer pull mid-POST:** pull the drawer right after a ball 10×; each goal arrives exactly once (409 on the repeat); re-insert boots clean with the queue intact.
12. **Mallet test:** drawer latched, tap the sled hard 20× during a session: no reset, no lost goal (contact bounce vs C1).
13. **RSSI in the table** with the lid on and the drawer closed: better than −75 dBm at 15 dBm; then with the STAT plug pulled (S6): if the difference is > 3 dB, apply C3.
14. **USB in place:** drawer out, slim cable along the lane wall; plug-in wakes it (`[usb] powered`), `-t upload` works, chirps still play (weaker on USB is fine), unplug → normal sleep.
15. **Measure for mechanical:** the pogo pin's barrel (dimensions.md L63), the OLED's active-area offset (L79), the FIT0610 lead exit.
16. **Write on the board label:** device_id, fitted R_LED / R_LED_B / R_PU, R_R / R_G, the clone's listing and the measured sleep current. Unit 2 copies them.

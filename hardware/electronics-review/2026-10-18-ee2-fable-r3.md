## Verdict

1. **OK to buy the first-table parts.** The cart (`bom.md` L19–69) is complete for two counters with spares and every
   line is an exact MPN that checks out against its maker's data. No finding in this round changes the order.
2. Every item from my round-2 file is closed, except two nits that are documentation only (table below). Nothing is open
   on the circuit.
3. The round-3 changes introduce no new electrical risk: the JST-PH pair and the fuse are right-sized (fuse I²t margin
   ≈ 20× on the hot-plug inrush, 13 mV at a TX burst), R_BS puts 1 kΩ between GPIO4 and anything plugged on J_BTN, the
   polarity of a cable cut in half is self-consistent, and D1 still makes a reversed pack harmless.
4. **What only a first unit on the bench can close** is listed in its own section: the cutoff (the README now says
   plainly that 3.65 V misses its own ≥ 3.1 V gate on paper at 15 dBm and sits on the line at 11 dBm), daylight margins
   and plan C, edge times, I²C rise time, RSSI at 11 dBm, sleep currents, the OLED's holes and offset.
5. Confidence unit 1 powers up, flashes and counts on the bench first time: **≈ 93 %**. Confidence it runs for months in
   the table as drawn: **≈ 85 %**, the residual being the cutoff-versus-months trade the bench will set and the daylight
   gate; both have a documented fallback (higher cutoff; `a_boost`/`b`, plan C).

## New findings from the round-3 changes

None are blockers and none change the cart. Ranked:

**S1 (should-fix, doc). The Waveshare module's 2.54 mm holes are an assumption.** Waveshare's page and wiki list the
interface as "PH2.0 connector" and never mention a header footprint; the new build steps (ASSEMBLY L437–441,
SLED-ASSEMBLY L110–111, dimensions L60 "2.54 holes at the LEFT end") depend on it, and mechanical's pocket and `OLED_FLIP`
0 depend on the wired end being the left end. *Fix:* add to the on-arrival list (SLED-ASSEMBLY §9) "confirm the four
2.54 holes and which end they are on", and give the fallback in ASSEMBLY §4.5: solder the four wires to the PH2.0
socket's own solder tails on the back (they are plain through-hole or SMT pins; 28 AWG takes to them), then the wired end
is the right end and `OLED_FLIP` goes back to 1. Either way the harness is the same four jumpers in the keyed 5-cavity
housing, so nothing is ordered differently.

**S2 (should-fix, doc). A blown F1 is now a failure mode with no symptom row.** Troubleshooting L580 lists a swapped or
broken splice and the switch bridge, not the fuse, and the fuse sits under heat-shrink behind the holder. *Fix:* add
"F1 open: meter across the shrink-wrapped fuse; a pinched lead blew it, find the pinch before replacing" to L580, and put
"F1 spares × 2" on the swap-kit note in §9 (the cart already buys 4 for 2 counters: fine).

**N1 (nice). Make the antenna keep-out an assert.** `perfboard.py` L49 states "≥ 5 mm from the XIAO's far (antenna)
edge" as a comment; `body_clashes()` doesn't check it. Three lines next to `MODULES` would stop the next re-layout from
putting a part back at 2 mm (round 2 did exactly that).

**N2 (nice). R_BTN's body touches the SparkFun outline in the model.** Standing over (14,2) (x 41.4, y 8.5) with the
modelled r 1.15 its top edge is at y 9.65 against the LIS3DH box at 9.7: 0.05 mm, inside the assert's 0.05 clearance.
A real MFR-25 body is Ø 2.3 ± 0.3, so lean it toward the bottom edge when soldering (ASSEMBLY L160 could say so), or
move it one row down to (14,1)–(13,1) if those holes stay free.

**N3 (nice). Fuse pulse budget, for the record.** Mating J_BAT (or inserting the last cell) charges C1 470 µF from ≈ 4.3 V
through D1, the fuse and ≈ 0.2 Ω of path: ≈ 15–20 A for ≈ 0.1 ms, I²t ≈ 0.02 A²s against 0.405 A²s melting (Littelfuse
251 series, 2 A), i.e. ≈ 5 % per event: no nuisance blow over the life of the table. Normal peaks (TX + chirp ≈ 0.5 A)
are a quarter of the rating. 47 mΩ cold gives 13 mV at 0.28 A, as the README's path table says.

**N4 (nice). Stale phrases** (owners): INTERFACES L71 and L80, ASSEMBLY L88 and L114 (N8 above); bom L36 still buys 3 ×
3-pin housings per counter where 2 are used (harmless).

**N5 (nice). SparkFun SEN-13963 spare** (bom L24, 2 for 2 counters): the one module with no spare; 63 kr against a
2–3 day wait if one is DOA.

## What only a first unit can close (bench or on arrival)

Ordered by how much they decide. Each is already a step in ASSEMBLY, README or the firmware checklist.

1. **The cutoff.** README L212–217 and L259–263: at 15 dBm the paper path drop (0.55–0.70 V) leaves 2.95–3.10 V at a
   3.65 V pack, under the ≥ 3.1 V gate; at 11 dBm below warn it sits on the line. The scope on 3V3 with the real holder,
   fuse, lead and JST pair, a High chirp over a POST, the OLED on and the final status, sets `LOW_BATT_CUTOFF_MV`. Every
   0.1 V higher costs ≈ 5–10 % of the months (README L257–258); that trade is the bench's to report, not a part change.
2. **Two-point VBAT calibration** to ±20 mV at 3.6 / 3.65 / 4.5 V (ASSEMBLY L477–481): the gate above is only as good as
   this.
3. **Daylight.** Lit / ambient / ball in all three modes at the worst hour, torch through the opening (ASSEMBLY L510–514);
   plan C on in real sun with 50 balls including hard ≈ 9 ms shots, its ≈ +5 mA, and off again (firmware README L246–248).
   The twin says 0/60 misses at 3 ms with the screen on; the bench confirms the 0.6 ms masked read and the 3 ms cadence
   with a scope on a GPIO (firmware README L126).
4. **Edge times** on D8 at 220 kΩ, which set `min_block_ms` (README L50–55).
5. **I²C rise time** at the far OLED before any move from 100 kHz to 400 kHz (`board.h` L32–33).
6. **RSSI in the table at 11 dBm** with the lid on, better than −75 dBm (≈ −71 read at 15 dBm), else the Molex FPC antenna
   (firmware README L234; the spot is on the lid).
7. **Sleep currents**: base ≤ 25 µA with the OLED off and blinks off; the full device averaged over ≥ 4 blink cycles; the
   OLED's own sleep current (its regulator isn't documented on Waveshare's page); the status-LED off-state current
   (INTERFACES L80 now says "measured on the bench").
8. **C1 leakage screening** (4 bought, fit the lowest; README L218–221).
9. **On arrival:** the Waveshare's 2.54 holes and which end (S1), its active-area offset and PH2.0 socket size
   (SLED-ASSEMBLY L254, L259), the FIT0610 outline and lead exit, the black filament's 940 nm opacity (SLED-ASSEMBLY
   L62–64), the TEFT4300 collector by meter before crimping.
10. **Mechanics with current in them:** the lead's U rolling 20× to the cord's stop with the lead still slack
    (SLED-ASSEMBLY L160–162), the mallet test during TX for contact bounce at the holder springs (README L226–227),
    the screen-on / 20-chirp / wiggle tests for false goals (ASSEMBLY L523–528), buzzer audibility with no grille.

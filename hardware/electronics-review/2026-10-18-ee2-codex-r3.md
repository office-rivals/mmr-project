## New should-fix findings from round 3

No new component-selection purchase blocker identified. These corrections are needed before relying on the assembly instructions or qualification claims.

### R3-S1 — The literal harness instructions add an unmodelled PVC tail

`ASSEMBLY.md:408–414` cuts the **500 mm** Adafruit 1131 cable in half, shortens **only the board half** to 30 mm, then splices the other half to **260 mm** of silicone. Followed literally, this makes roughly **510 mm** from plug to holder joint, instead of the mechanical guide's **~260 mm total** (`mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md:75–76`) and model's ~247 mm path. The extra tail is **26 AWG PVC**, not the modeled silicone rolling pair. These material/length specifications are confirmed by [Adafruit 1131](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1131) and [Adafruit 6181](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6181).

**Fix:** explicitly shorten the plug half too; specify finished connector-to-joint length, splice positions and allowances. Keep the PVC tail and rigid heat-shrunk splices in the stationary, strain-relieved section, with silicone through the rolling bend. Dry-fit before final cutting. Update the remaining LEAD_LEN252 references in `dimensions.md:72` and `wiring.yml:91` to the current ~247 geometry. Parts already ordered suffice.

### R3-S2 — Cord clearance assertions do not establish the claimed stop

`sled.py:244–253` retains a **semicircular r7 cord bend even at 125 mm extension**. At that pose the upper straight segment vanishes, but the curved segment does not. From the actual endpoints, the modeled free path is approximately **164.3 + π×7 = 186.29 mm**, whereas the direct endpoint separation is **sqrt(164.3² + 14²) = 164.90 mm**. There is about **21.4 mm** of excess path, not a demonstrated taut cord.

The assertion at `sled.py:553–559` checks constant straight-segment length and zero upper segment, not tension or anchor-to-anchor length. Thus the owner report's “runs out of U at125” is not proof that the cord arrests travel there. This does **not** invalidate the guide's better instruction: `mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md:157–159` says to tie the cord **taut at125**. That creates a different free length/path from the displayed model.

**Fix:** reconcile the cord model with the tied free length, including anchor routing, and recheck its changing path against the cable. Retain the guide's physical stop-setting procedure. Verify with actual pulling that the cord takes load before conductors or splices, and that the shorter cord stows without snagging. No new electrical part is required.

### R3-S3 — Receive RSSI / TX-power conversion — closed during this review

The initially inspected `firmware/README.md` required RSSI >−75 dBm at11 dBm TX and called that approximately −71 dBm at15 dBm. The station's RSSI measures the **AP's received beacon**, not its own transmitted signal; changing station TX power does not impose that four-dB change. See [Espressif's ESP32-C6 Wi-Fi API, esp_wifi_sta_get_rssi](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32c6/api-reference/network/esp_wifi.html#_CPPv422esp_wifi_sta_get_rssiPi).

**Final re-read:** the current README's Network gate and Power section now explicitly distinguish the AP downlink from local TX and require delivered requests, retries and reconnects at11 dBm. The test-definition defect is **closed**. Installed qualification remains open: exercise actual goal/status delivery and latency at both ends with people/balls/cells present. Any return to15 dBm or fitting of the FPC fallback must repeat both RF and low-rail power qualification.

The added fuse itself presents no identified purchase problem: Littelfuse's nominal cold resistance is **0.0473 Ω**, giving about **13 mV at0.28 A**, consistent with the owner's approximate14 mV allowance. Keep it at the holder and insulated. Its purpose is short-circuit protection; mechanical pinch prevention still matters. [Littelfuse 251/253 datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse_fuse_251_253_datasheet.pdf?assetguid=f47a0bb7-8ede-4679-9646-7114c3787688).

# Goal counter: independent hardware review (ee2-opus, 2026-10-18)

Scope: the goal counter only, judged from `electronics/` (README, bom, schematic.py, perfboard.py, wiring.yml,
dimensions), `ASSEMBLY.md`, `mechanical/SLED-ASSEMBLY.md` + renders, `firmware/include/board.h` + README and
INTERFACES §0/§2–§4, against the datasheets listed at the end. No design history read.

## Verdict

1. **Buy after these fixes.** The circuit itself has no electrical blocker. The blockers are gaps in the cart (cheap)
   and an unnamed pogo pin. Two small hardware changes (S1 keyed J_BAT, S2 gold targets) change the order, so make
   them first.
2. **Confidence it works first time** (powers up, flashes, counts goals, reports over Wi-Fi): **≈ 80–85 % as
   specified, ≥ 95 % with the fixes.** What's left is environmental: daylight in the drawer and Wi-Fi inside the
   table. Both have fallbacks: the beam modes, and a U.FL antenna (S4).
3. **The netlist is right.** I scripted a check: every perfboard hole maps to the schematic net and the firmware pin.
   The C6 pin choices (LP GPIO, ADC, straps, per-pin ext1) are correct, and the XIAO power path matches Seeed's KiCad
   files.
4. **Main hardware risks:**
   - the battery plug fits every other header, and some of those would fry the XIAO (S1);
   - gold pogo pins press on bare nickel (S2);
   - the live, flush targets on a pulled drawer can be shorted (S3);
   - the antenna sits behind the cells (S4).
5. **Expect the low-battery cutoff to rise to about 3.6–3.7 V.** During a TX burst the 3V3 rail sits about 0.55–0.7 V
   below the resting pack voltage, not the README's ~0.4 V (S5). That costs ~5–10 % of capacity, which still leaves
   ≈ 1.7× margin over the 1-month target even in the pessimistic budget.

## What I checked and found correct

- **Perfboard netlist (scripted from `perfboard.py`).** Every hole is on exactly one net, and every part pin is on its
  intended net. The XIAO pins match README:264–283 and `board.h`. D1, D3 and C1 polarity are right, and Q2 is C-B-E
  from the USB end, flat side to the bottom edge (correct for the BC337).
- **ESP32-C6 pin use (datasheet v1.5).**
  - LP GPIO are GPIO0–7 (Table 2-7), so INT1, BTN, VBUS_S and STAT can all wake the chip or be driven by the LP core.
  - ADC1 is on GPIO0–6, so D0 and D2 are fine.
  - MTMS and MTDI only strap SDIO edge timing, which is harmless. GPIO8, 9 and 15 are untouched.
  - MTDO (buzzer), MTMS and MTDI have **no** pulls at or after reset (Table 2-1), so the 100 k R_BD keeps the buzzer
    silent at boot.
  - Per-pin ext1 levels are supported: `SOC_PM_SUPPORT_EXT1_WAKEUP_MODE_PER_PIN (1)` in ESP-IDF's esp32c6 `soc_caps.h`.
- **XIAO power path (Seeed KiCad v1.0, 2026-01-14).**
  - BAT+ goes to the SGM40567 charger BAT pin (ICHG = 24000 / 200 k = 120 mA) and to an LP0404N3T5G PMOS into the
    SGM6029CYG buck. The PMOS gate is VBUS through 100 Ω, so it is on when USB is absent.
  - VBUS reaches the buck through an LMBR4010B Schottky. The header 5V pin is raw VBUS with a 100 k pull-down, so it
    sits at ≈ 0 V on batteries, as README:283 says.
  - GPIO4–7 go straight to back test points with no parts on them. GPIO3 drives the RF-switch PMOS through a 10 k
    pull-up. A U.FL connector is fitted (GPIO14 selects it).
- **Buck.** The SGM6029C runs from 1.95–5.5 V (6 V absolute), has 2.3 µA Iq and a 100 % duty mode. A fresh L91 pack
  (≈ 5.3 V after D1) fits.
- **Optics.** TEFT4300: Ica 0.8 min / 3.2 typ mA at 1 mW/cm², ICEO ≤ 200 nA, VECO 5 V. TSAL6200: 40 / 72 / 200 mW/sr
  at 100 mA, ±17°, VR 5 V. The README's 6.5× typical and 0.91× worst-case margins in mode `a` hold. I'd shave the
  typical to ~3–5× for vignetting at the Ø3 apertures and lower efficiency at 1 mA. Worst-case parts are still
  rescued by `a_boost` (≈ 3.7×) or R_LED = 470 Ω.
- **Status LEDs.** The one-pin red/green trick works: series Vf at µA levels is > 3.3 V, so nothing leaks at Hi-Z.
- **Buzzer.** The CMT-1209-390T is a passive magnetic transducer (2048 Hz, 3.0 Vo-p rated, 2–5 V, 65 mA, 13–19 Ω),
  correctly driven low-side from VSYS with a 1N5817 flyback. A 2.5 mA base current saturates a BC337-40 at these
  currents.
- **I²C.** The two modules' pull-ups in parallel give ≈ 2.3–5 kΩ, fine at 400 kHz with the fallback to 100 kHz.
- **Unkeyed passive leads plugged backwards are all harmless.** The LED sees 3.3 V reverse against VR 5 V. The
  TEFT4300 sees 3.3 V against VECO 5 V. The button works either way. A reversed status-LED lead only reverse-biases
  the LEDs through 680 / 220 Ω. The sensor plug at the mouth joint is keyed.

## Findings

### Blockers (fix in the order)

**B1. The counted cart can't build two counters.**
- Missing or short:
  - **R_G 220 Ω: none in the cart.** It lists no 220 Ω value at all (bom.md:77–100).
  - **R_R 680 Ω.** The four 680 Ω are R_LED_B × 2 plus spares (bom.md:89).
  - **150 Ω and 330 Ω**, needed for the green tuning range in ASSEMBLY.md:477.
  - **4-pin Dupont housings for J_OLED: none.**
  - **Machined socket.** Two boards need 20 pins (R_LED, R_LED_B, R_PU and R_G/R_R are 10 per board), but the cart
    has one 1 × 20 strip (bom.md:99), which leaves zero margin for cut losses.
  - **Male headers.** Each counter needs ≈ 20 pins: J_PT, J_LED, J_BAT, J_BTN, J_BZ, J_OLED, J_STAT and the 3-pin
    mouth joint. The cart has one 1 × 40 strip. bom.md:200 still says "needs 6".
  - **XIAO headers.** bom.md:200 and ASSEMBLY.md:72 assume the XIAO ships with its 2 × 7 headers. Seeed's wiki says it
    ships without headers by default, and resellers sell a separate "with headers" variant. That is 28 more pins.
- Jumper counts disagree: 19 per counter (bom.md:39/201), 16 (ASSEMBLY.md:333), 10 (bom.md:72) and 6 (bom.md:96,
  ASSEMBLY.md:82). 19 is right, so two packs leave only 2 spares.
- **Fix:** add to order A:
  - resistors: 220 Ω × 4, 680 Ω × 2, 150 Ω × 2, 330 Ω × 2;
  - housings: 4-pin × 4, and 3-pin to ≥ 8 in total (J_STAT, the sensor plug and the keyed J_BAT of S1);
  - headers: machined 1 × 20 × 2 more, male 1 × 40 × 3;
  - jumpers: a third 20-pack;
  - the XIAO "with headers" variant, or count its pins.

**B2. The pogo pin is "pick at order" (bom.md:40, :202).**
- The obvious Mill-Max solder-cup pin, **0933**, is standard stroke: 0.055" (1.40 mm) maximum, which fails the
  ≥ 1.5 mm spec.
- **Fix:** name **Mill-Max 0973-0-15-20-77-14-11-0**: solder cup up to 20 AWG, ≈ 2.0 mm maximum and ≈ 1.0 mm working
  stroke, 60 g at mid-stroke, 2 A, ≤ 20 mΩ, gold.
- Then set the stand-offs to ~1.0 mm compression. The present 0.75 mm (dimensions.md:64) gives only ≈ 50 g on this
  pin. Measure the barrel for sled.py's Ø2.5 hole (dimensions.md:63). Buy 10 if you take S2's two-pins-per-pole
  option.

### Should-fix

**S1. Key J_BAT: as drawn, the battery plug fits every other header, and every lead fits J_BAT.**
- J_BAT is a plain 2-pin header in a row of 2-pin headers (perfboard.py:41–42, 51–53). SLED-ASSEMBLY:93 plugs them
  all on the bench with the drawer out, so a mistake only bites when the drawer goes in (SLED-ASSEMBLY:132).
- **Battery plug on the wrong header:**
  - on J_PT, 3.6–4.4 V goes straight into GPIO19 and GPIO2 (no series R) and back-feeds the 3V3 rail through their
    ESD diodes;
  - on J_BTN, the same happens into GPIO4;
  - on J_OLED's V pin, 4.4 V lands on the 3V3 rail (C6 maximum 3.6 V, Table 5-2);
  - on J_STAT's S pin, it goes straight into GPIO5.
- **Other leads on J_BAT:**
  - the TSAL6200 lead puts the LED across the pack with no resistor, which means amps through 28 AWG;
  - the buzzer lead puts 16 Ω across the pack: ≈ 0.27 A continuous against a 65 mA rating;
  - the status-LED and OLED leads would kill their parts.
- **Fix:**
  - Make J_BAT a 3-position header with the **middle pin pulled**: + at (17,0), no pin at (18,0), − at (19,0). (19,0)
    is free, and the GND hop (18,0)→(20,0) becomes (19,0)→(20,0).
  - Fit the battery lead in a **3-cavity housing with the middle cavity plugged**, the same trick as the sensor plug.
- No other housing can then make two contacts on J_BAT, and the battery plug can't make two contacts anywhere else.
  It costs two 3-pin housings.

**S2. Gold pins on bare nickel will creep.**
- The pogo pins press ≈ 0.5 N axially (no wipe) onto 0.15 mm nickel strip (bom.md:202, ASSEMBLY.md:382–391). Mill-Max
  (PR665) specifies mating with **gold-plated pads or Mill-Max targets**. Low force, a mixed gold/nickel interface and
  table vibration mean fretting. Contact resistance creeps, and at TX peaks a few hundred mΩ more is a brown-out (S5
  has no margin).
- **Fix:** use gold targets (8 × 8 mm squares cut from an ENIG-finish PCB or protoboard, with the holder lead
  soldered to the back), and/or **2 pins per pole**. Count brown-out resets (reset_reason) in telemetry as the field
  monitor.

**S3. A pulled drawer exposes live, flush targets 10 mm apart.**
- The targets sit flush on the drawer's rear (dimensions.md:64, SLED-ASSEMBLY:119) and connect straight to 3 × NiMH.
  A key, coin, knife or metal tabletop across them is a 10–20 A short through 26 AWG: hot leads and a melted drawer.
- **Fix (sled.py):** sink each target ≥ 1 mm into its own pocket that only the pin's plunger reaches, and re-set the
  stand-offs. A 1 A PTC also works but costs 0.05–0.1 V at TX.

**S4. Wi-Fi: the antenna is behind the cells.**
- The XIAO's chip antenna (board end, s ≈ 140–144) sits ~20–25 mm behind the three cells and the pogo pins, which lie
  between it and the frame opening.
- The J_OLED and J_PT plugs and their leads rise 12–15 mm from the antenna end (J_OLED at s 143–151,
  dimensions.md:58).
- Nobody has measured RSSI in the table.
- **Fix:** buy **2 × U.FL 2.4 GHz FPC antennas** now (≈ 20 kr; the XIAO has the U.FL, GPIO14 HIGH selects it). Have
  mechanical leave a stick-on spot on the dash or lid. Decide after the in-table RSSI test (bench item 9). Route the
  OLED and J_PT leads away from the XIAO's far end.

**S5. The brown-out budget misses ~0.25 V.**
- README:169 says the rail holds 3.3 V above a 3.45 V pack, and README:205–208 puts it at ≈ 3.0–3.05 V during TX at
  the cutoff. That counts only the cells and the diode.
- The full path at a 0.28 A burst (15 dBm TX + CPU):

  | Element | Drop at 0.28 A |
  |---|---|
  | Cells | ≈ 0.05 V |
  | FIT0610 springs + switch | 0.03–0.07 V |
  | Leads, nickel, pogo pins, J_BAT Dupont | 0.03–0.04 V |
  | 1N5817 (onsemi: ≤ 0.32 V at 0.1 A, ≤ 0.45 V at 1 A) | ≈ 0.33–0.40 V |
  | XIAO PMOS | 0.01–0.03 V |
  | SGM6029C at 100 % duty (high side 185 mΩ typ, 310 mΩ max, + inductor DCR) | 0.065–0.1 V |
  | **Total** | **≈ 0.55–0.70 V** |

- At a 3.45 V pack the rail dips to **≈ 2.75–2.9 V**. That is below the C6's 3.0 V minimum (Table 5-2) and close to
  the in-package flash's **2.7 V minimum** (Table 5-12).
- **Fix:**
  - Scope it with the real holder, pogo pins and J_BAT lead in the path. A stiff supply at J_BAT hides 0.1–0.2 V, and
    the firmware README's ramp test doesn't say where its supply connects.
  - Expect `LOW_BATT_CUTOFF_MV` ≈ 3600–3700 (board.h:12–14).
  - Firmware: no NVS writes and no chirp during TX near cutoff, and a brown-out detector level ≥ 2.7 V.

**S6. On USB alone, D0 can read a "pack".**
- With the drawer out, which is the only way USB is plugged in the field (ASSEMBLY.md:523), the charger holds the BAT
  pad at ~4.1–4.2 V. D1's reverse leakage flows into the 2 MΩ divider.
- The 1N5817 is rated ≤ 1 mA at 20 V, and its typical-reverse plot bottoms out at 1 µA. **1 µA lifts VPACK to ≈ 2 V
  and D0 to 1.0 V**, which is board.h's `NO_PACK_MV`. README:190 says "about 0 V".
- **Fix:** firmware ignores VBAT while VBUS_S is HIGH (no cutoff, battery_mv null). Bench item 2 measures it.

**S7. The sleep-current acceptance contradicts the budget.**
- ASSEMBLY.md:498 says "≤ 30 µA". The same device adds the OLED in 0xAE (5–20 µA, README:239) and the LP-core blinks
  (2 mA × 30 ms every 5 s ≈ 12 µA average, README:240) to the ≈ 22 µA base (README:231). A builder will fail a good
  board and go hunting for a clone fault.
- **Fix:** measure the base with the OLED unplugged and blinks off (≤ 25 µA), then the full device: the baseline
  between blinks should be ≤ ~40 µA.

**S8. Plugs and sockets need securing against vibration.**
- There are 8 friction-only Dupont plugs, two of them in series on the 220 kΩ beam node (J_PT and the mouth joint),
  plus 5 socketed resistors.
- If R_LED, R_PU, J_LED or the sensor path opens for longer than `min_block_ms`, it reads as a ball: a false goal.
  If J_BAT or J_PT works loose, the unit dies.
- **Fix:** after bench calibration, put a dab of hot glue on each housing-to-header joint and each socketed resistor
  (it peels off for service). Tie each lead down within ~20 mm of its plug.

**S9. ASSEMBLY's per-device parts list (ASSEMBLY.md:72–88) predates phase 1.**
- It lists 6 jumpers, 3 × 2 header pins and a 1 × 4 socket.
- It has no 680 Ω, 22 kΩ, 1 nF, second 1N5817, BC337, buzzer, MX switch, OLED, status LEDs, pogo pins or nickel
  strip.
- **Fix:** regenerate it from the corrected cart (B1).

**S10. The FIT0610's slide switch stays in the power path.**
- It is "ON, unused" (bom.md:194, ASSEMBLY.md:389): a cheap series contact that vibration can open and a cell swap can
  knock off.
- **Fix:** solder a bridge across its lugs, or use a holder without a switch.

### Nice

- **N1. MTCK (GPIO6) comes out of reset with its pull-up on** (Table 2-1 note 5, while `EFUSE_DIS_PAD_JTAG` = 0).
  - Against the 68 k / 100 k divider (40.5 kΩ to 0 V on batteries), it parks the pin at ≈ 1.56 V, an undefined level,
    and draws ≈ 39 µA until firmware clears it. The same happens in deep sleep if the LP pad isn't configured.
  - Firmware: `rtc_gpio_pullup_dis(6)` before sleep.
  - Hardware option: **6.8 k / 10 k** gives the same 2.98 V on USB and stays a clean LOW even with the pull-up on.
- **N2. R_STRAP is tight against the strong end of the pull-up range.** 10 kΩ against the 45 kΩ pull-up gives 0.60 V
  against VIL ≤ 0.825 V; against a ~30 kΩ pull-up it gives ≈ 0.83 V. **4.7 kΩ** gives 0.31 V.
- **N3. Fresh L91 overdrives the buzzer.** It gives VSYS ≈ 5.3 V, above the CMT-1209's 5.0 Vo-p operating maximum.
  NiMH and alkaline are fine. Cap the duty when VBAT > 5 V.
- **N4. Name exact MPNs for the status LEDs and the OLED.** The LEDs are a 3 mm diffused InGaN green at 520–530 nm and
  an AlInGaP red (bom.md:207 gives only a spec).
  - The LIS3DH clone rule "no regulator" is stricter than needed: an XC6206-class LDO draws ~1 µA. Let the sleep meter
    decide.
  - The SparkFun fallback's pin order (GND VCC SDA SCL SA0 CS / GND ADC1-3 INT1 INT2) matches perfboard.py:34–36.
- **N5. Hot-glue some parts against vibration.** The standing C1 can (Ø6.3 × 11 on two leads) and the MX switch's
  resistor joint.
- **N6. Doc nits:**
  - ASSEMBLY §1, §6 and §8 point to `mechanical/print-and-install.md` (the old insert), not SLED-ASSEMBLY.md.
  - ASSEMBLY.md:113 says "two printed pins"; dimensions.md:14 says one pin and a boss.
  - board.h:35–37 still says "12 x 12 tactile" and "GPIO5 stays spare".
  - README:296–306 still has the LED on D3.
  - wiring.yml LS1 mentions a "grille"; as built there is none.
- **N7. BOOT is out of reach in the sled.** If firmware ever disables USB-Serial-JTAG, recovery means pulling the sled.
  That's acceptable; just know it.

## Challenges (what I'd do differently)

1. **A PCB for units 2–6**, once the first perfboard unit proves the circuit.
   - Make it a 2-layer 70 × 30 board with the same, clean netlist and keyed JST sockets for every lead.
   - It removes ~110 hand joints and the whole cross-plug class of mistakes. Expect ~1–2 weeks and ~150 kr for 5–10
     boards.
   - The back-pad wires stay.
2. **Swap the beam sides**: put the receiver on L beside the board and the emitter on R.
   - The 220 kΩ node becomes a short local lead, and the 210 mm J_PT extension through the duct (beside the buzzer and
     OLED leads) disappears.
   - The long R-to-L cable then carries a 1–4 mA LED drive that shrugs off crosstalk.
   - It's a mechanical rework, so it's worth it only if the PCB happens. C_RX (1 nF) already protects the node
     adequately.
3. **A daylight plan C with no new hardware.** The LED is on a GPIO and the node on the ADC, so firmware can chop the
   LED and subtract on/off readings when `ambient_high` persists.
   - At 20 kΩ the node settles in ~50–100 µs, so 2–5 kHz chopping works.
   - It costs ≈ +3–5 mA while active.
4. **An ideal diode instead of the 1N5817.** An LM66100-class part (~80 mΩ, nA Iq, blocks reverse current) wins back
   ≈ 0.3 V at TX, so the rail stays in spec down to a ≈ 3.35 V pack.
   - It's SMD on a breakout, so do it only if S5's scope forces the cutoff above ~3.7 V.
   - Don't use a plain P-FET: it would let the XIAO's charger charge the AAs whenever USB and cells meet.
5. **Start the first table in `a_boost` or `b`, not `a`.** Mode `a` (220 k, 1 mA) is the most ambient- and
   leak-sensitive mode: a ball registers only if stray light stays < 3.75 µA, ≈ 5 % of the typical lit current. It
   also has the slowest edges (~1–2 ms). Step down once the health data shows margin. This is config only.

## Bench checklist: first unit, before building the second

1. **Before power.** Run the ASSEMBLY 3.8 continuity list. Check that no other plug makes two contacts on J_BAT (S1).
2. **USB only, drawer out.**
   - 3V3 reads 3.25–3.35 V and nothing gets warm. VBUS_S reads ≈ 3 V.
   - **Record D0**: the firmware must ignore it while USB is in (S6).
   - Reflashing through the lane works, and plugging USB wakes the board.
3. **Drawer in, USB out.** J_BAT+ = pack. C1+ = pack − 0.1–0.2 V. D0 = pack / 2. 5V pin < 0.5 V.
4. **Sleep current.**
   - OLED unplugged, blinks off: ≤ 25 µA.
   - Full device: baseline between blinks ≤ ~40 µA, with no extra tens of µA from GPIO6 or the beam pins.
5. **Brown-out.**
   - Put the real holder, pogo pins and J_BAT lead in the path.
   - Scope 3V3 at the XIAO during TX with the buzzer on High and the OLED on.
   - Find the pack voltage at which 3V3 dips below 3.0 V, and set the cutoff from it (S5).
6. **Drawer contacts.** < 0.2 Ω each. 20 insert/remove cycles. Mallet or vibration during TX bursts gives zero
   resets (check reset_reason).
7. **Beam.**
   - Record lit, ambient and ball levels (µA) in modes `a`, `a_boost` and `b`, with room lights on and in the worst
     daylight hour, with a ball held in the gate and lit by a torch through the opening.
   - Scope the edges at 220 k and 20 k.
   - Count 50 match-speed goals exactly, plus hard shots.
8. **False goals.** Play 10 min hard with no ball reaching the gate, and wiggle J_LED, J_PT, the mouth joint and every
   socketed resistor: zero GOAL events.
9. **RSSI in the real table.** Drawer in, lid on, at both goal ends: ≥ −75 dBm at 15 dBm. Otherwise fit the U.FL
   antenna (S4).
10. **I²C.** No LIS3DH or OLED errors at 400 kHz while the buzzer plays.
11. **Buzzer.** Audible from both playing positions in a noisy room.
12. **Wake.** Motion, button and USB all wake it. One hour untouched with no spurious wake.
13. **Cell swaps.** Five swaps through the frame: it boots every time and the queue survives.
